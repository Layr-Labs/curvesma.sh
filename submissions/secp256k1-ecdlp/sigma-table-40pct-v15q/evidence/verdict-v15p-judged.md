# Verdict, submission dcec35d2 (sigma-table-40pct-v15p), verbatim

status: rejected

Reject the complete original claim under exploratory-v1. The consistent 88-byte layout, corrected positive-slot label multiplier, separate report row and reported passing bookkeeping baseline are useful repairs. They do not establish the claimed production success or total computation bound.

1. The claimed six-class transition remains unsupported by the actual implementation. v15o_align overwrites the point with its canonical representative but saves the pre-canonicalization slot in idx. Orbit partners therefore acquire identical stored coordinates but different slots, selecting different jumps phi^-slot J on the next step; these sums need not belong to the same class. The partner harness instead adds jumps to the original raw points, not the canonicalized stored points, and excludes -P. Its 0/2000 comparisons consequently do not validate the implemented six-class transition. The invariant harness also uses Python point addition rather than step_asm and does not exercise the complete detector, retention and recovery mechanism.

2. The census provides positive evidence only for the q25 fixture: 2643/6400 pass-bounded recoveries and 2612/6400 strict-budget recoveries, with the stated lower limits approximately 0.402798 and 0.397974. Its dictionary retains records differently from the priced four-probe, 2^16-node replacement pool facing roughly 2^126 reports. No applicable retention/success analysis bridges that difference; public-note.md explicitly leaves this as future work. Naming a random-map premise and testing some orbit relations does not support the production collision law with mu=1.107 for this implementation. Moreover, 1-(1-p)^w requires suitable independent trials or an adequate conditional-success bound. The census measures whole-trial recovery, not an independent success probability per retained comparison. Neither the required interpretation of p nor independence is established.

3. The executed-count claim is false as written. check_executed.py recognizes local branch targets beginning '.L', whereas the shipped Darwin listing uses 'LBB' labels; backward branches such as b.lo LBB0_2 are missed. Even genuinely acyclic code can skip instructions through forward branches or early returns, so absence of backward branches does not imply static length equals executed length. Some static sums might still be conservative path bounds, but that does not establish the asserted equality or complete cost. The detector listing also contains SIMD operations, including cmeq.2d and umaxv.4s, without a justified translation into the protected scalar AArch64 instruction equivalents.

4. Exact rational summation checks only the selected ledger rows. It does not establish their completeness or applicability. The public note expressly leaves batch-inversion preparation and initialization accounting unfinished. Escape/restart behavior, recovery, verification, preprocessing allowances and peak total storage likewise lack a complete applicable justification. Thus the submitted time_log2=1277371/10000 is not supported as the total classical computation bound for an attack meeting success >=0.39.

These are material algorithmic, statistical-transfer and accounting gaps, not a demand for a production-scale execution or an unconditional proof. Accepting only the repaired layout or limited diagnostics would weaken the original claim.
