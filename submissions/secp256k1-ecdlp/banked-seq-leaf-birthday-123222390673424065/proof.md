## 1. Overview

On secp256k1 (y^2=x^3+7 over F_p, p=2^256-2^32-977, prime order r, phi(x,y)=(beta x, y)=[lambda](x,y)), Phase 0 mines 8 points X_(p,0..7) in 4 pairs (0<delta_A_q=A_(p,2q+1)-A_(p,2q)<2^63, A=y^2 mod p) per pool across N_POOLS=2^14 GF(1024) pools, of which Phases 1-5 use MX=6 (X_(p,0..5), 3 pairs) to label 6 leaves x(phi^t(X) +- Y) (t=0,1,2) from 1/D (D=y_Y^2-y_X^2) via (Ew-b)+beta^t(Fw-a)+beta^(2t) Gw, with E, F, G held as K' Eisenstein pairs (3.3). A computed leaf's record goes into a direct-mapped hash table of 2^132 slots (2^85 banks of 2^47 slots), unless a cap of success.md 6.4 drops it; an insert that meets a stored record with the same 169-bit label lists the pair, and Phase 4 resolves listed pairs for x.

What the processor executes for a select. DSB SY; STP of the two bank-number words to the window's select register; DSB SY. ## 8. Limitations

Limitations. (1) Premise BM (premises table): the memory device's existence at this size is assumed, and the count holds nothing for its selection of a bank beyond the three instructions of a select; the sensitivity line prices an addition to each hash select. (2) FEAT_CMPBR has no shipping implementation, and DCZID_EL0.BS=9 is IMPLEMENTATION DEFINED: hash zeroing (ledger row 9 and Phase 0's pass) assumes 2,048-byte DC ZVA blocks, and with 64-byte blocks a pass of the same form takes 4,098 instructions (2,048 DC ZVA with their ADDs, SUBS, B.NE) in place of 130 and the claim would be 123.4438 (certificate.py c56). (3) RNDR is taken as an ideal coin source (IC). (4) The claim is an expectation; the every-run ceiling is about 47.3 bits above it, because a walk is bounded only by a bank's overflow capacity. (5) The experiments run the kernels at small scale: production bank-number and slot widths on sparse memory, with at most 648 records on a machine; production-scale bounds are analytic (certificate.py). (6) Only the kernels are executed code. The driver between kernel calls, set-up other than the executed passes, Phase 4 (RESOLVE, REQ_SERVE) and the loops over banks, generators and rounds are analytic allowances (section 7 lists them).
