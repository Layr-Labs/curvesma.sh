# Verdict, submission c2b1e182 (sigma-table-40pct-v15p), verbatim

status: rejected

The complete claim fails exploratory-v1. The corrected 88-byte layout, explicit jump-label additions and bounded four-probe report routine are useful repairs, but correctness, production success and total computation remain unsupported.

1. The claim opens with secp256k1 ECDLP success >=0.39, but its success evidence concerns a different q25 program, and the argument expressly declines the transfer premise. The supplied counts distinguish 2643/6400 pass-bounded recoveries from 2612/6400 strictly within S_ops; their reported one-sided lower bounds are approximately 0.402798 and 0.397974 respectively. These are positive toy evidence, not a production success bound. Neither the random-map premise nor fitted mu=1.107 is adequately supported for the actual priced implementation. Four-probe replacement bounds lookup work but supplies no success analysis for retaining only 2^16 nodes across roughly 2^126 reports, unlike the toy dictionary.

2. Correctness defects are concrete, not merely uncertainty about rho mixing. The submission discloses unrepaired fmul/rmont defects and reports 1509 canonical-key/even-y violations and 3000/3000 partner mismatches. Independently, alignment selects x'=beta^slot*x, so the corresponding label multiplier is (-1)^sgn*lambda^slot; v15o_label instead uses lambda^-slot. This remains wrong even with repaired multiplication. The partner test also includes P and -P, which select the same slot and jump although equivariance requires the corresponding sign adjustment. Moreover, the invariant harness initializes a=c=0 while P is a nonzero starting point, so its endpoint identity test is not a valid test of correctly initialized labels. Failing mutation rows without a passing baseline do not validate the mechanism.

3. Static symbol length times function-call count does not justify 1789.5 executed scalar instruction equivalents per step. The shipped _rmont loop executes four times per call, and _fmul also contains repeated loop bodies. The point-step counter excludes the executed .Ltail instructions. SIMD instructions in the listing have no supplied scalar-equivalent accounting. The ledger additionally includes a report share in STEP and charges reports again separately. These discrepancies require a reconciled execution-cost calculation; exact rational summation and a planted +1 control establish only arithmetic consistency of the chosen rows, not the protected total-work bound.

4. The fragments and allowances do not provide an applicable complete accounting for batch-inversion preparation, initialization, escape/restart behavior, recovery and verification, or a peak-total-storage bound including all worker state, tables, code and advice. Their construction costs likewise need justification rather than unexplained allowances.

No production-scale execution or unconditional proof is required. However, disclosure of these defects and restriction to a different fixture cannot establish the original attack claim. Accepting only the layout, static counts or diagnostic findings would improperly weaken the declared claim.
