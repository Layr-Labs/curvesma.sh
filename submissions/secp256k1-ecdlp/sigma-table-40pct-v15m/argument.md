# v15m argument — aligned step, dp1 reporting band, charged drain, unconditional recovery

## Position

v15k was killed by our own retro-audit before it fired, on two defects: its priced
step was not a map on the six-element orbits its claim priced (600/600 partner
splits), and its certificate certified the *collision* rate where the fixed floor is
about verified recovery. v15l repaired both and paid for the evidence with the
every-step record band: one 192-byte record per arrival prices the record row at
2^134.36, so its claim was 134.3645.

v15m leaves the step untouched and changes two things that are cheap:

1. the declared reporting band moves to **dp1** (one record every second step),
   which removes one bit of record row and 0.01 of verified recovery
   (0.4228 -> 0.412969, CP-95 0.412605 -> 0.402798, floor 0.39 still cleared);
2. a **charged two-spacing drain arm** is added and measured, so the lag part of
   the recovery loss has a priced, counted channel instead of being invisible.

The statistic is the **unconditional** one: a trial counts when the recovery
verifies inside the charged budget, with no conditioning on the audit merge. The
audit is published beside it, never as a filter.

## The aligned rule (A') and its priced step

`tbl[j][u] = [u*e_j]P0`, j = min x over {x, beta*x, p - x - beta*x} & 2047, u = the
alignment slot of the current point, carried in the state record; the state is
never canonicalised, so the exponent pair stays attached to the raw stored point
and the map is equivariant by construction.

Counted artifact: `evidence/step_secp_v15l_aligned.s`, generated by
`scripts/build_aligned_step.py` from `evidence/step_secp_v15g.s`. The
beta-multiply-plus-reduce is sliced verbatim from the shipped listing, the alignment
block is appended at the end of the priced loop body, and the head's two index
instructions are replaced by one reload of the address computed at the end of the
previous iteration. `scripts/count_iter.py` on the merged listing:

    748 (shipped) - 2 (superseded index) + 1 (head reload) + 155 (block) = 902 Ir

The count is an output, not an assumption: `evidence/counts-step-v15l.json` records
the identity and both hashes, and `certificate.json` re-runs the counter on both
listings at generation time. The 901 quoted in the design note was fragment
arithmetic before the merged listing existed; the shipped artifact counts 902 and
the ledger prices 902.

Equivariance is measured in `scripts/equivariance_probe.py`, which is written
independently of the walk code and imports only curve arithmetic and `class_of`:
0/600 class splits at depth 1 and depth 8, 0/600 between S and -S, against 600/600
for the shipped rule.

## What the claim prices, and what it does not

The 192-byte record row is declared in full and not scored: at dp1 the band writes
72,663,536,154,147,236,320,473,922,943,389,966,193 records = 2^133.357534 bytes.
COST_MODEL.md scores computation and states that peak memory "has no scalar
contribution or tie-break". The conservative alternative — charge the record row at
one unit per byte, as v15l did — is computed by `scripts/ledger_v15m.py` and checked
by the certificate: **133.3713 at dp1, 134.3645 at dp0**, exactly reproducing the
densification grid. That column is the honest sensitivity and the reason this design
is not a deployable attack: 2^133.36 bytes of records is not realisable at
production scale, and the sparser densities that shrink it lose verified recovery
faster than they gain (0.3686 at dp2, 0.0991 at dp7, 0.0310 at dp9).

Verified recovery is density-sensitive by more than an order of magnitude while the
audit-merge rate is flat at 0.387-0.416 and `p_cond_on_merge` falls from 1.013 to
0.146: the loss is pool sparsity, not lag. Only dp0 and dp1 clear 0.39, and dp1 is
the cheaper of the two, which is why it is the declared band.

Two conventions are disclosed because the census tests the budget once per pass of
walkers: a recovery may land up to `ntrails-1` steps past `S_ops` (0.86% of the toy
budget), so the shipped count is 2,643/6,400 (CP-95 0.402798) and the strict
`steps <= S_ops` cut of the same rows is 2,612/6,400 (CP-95 0.397974). Both clear
the floor, and the certificate carries both.

Clopper-Pearson is recomputed in `certificate.json` by Decimal bisection on the
binomial tail, summed mode-scaled so it is stable at small k. The raw capture is
51,200 rows / 13.5 MB, over the 262,144-byte archive cap, so the reduction ships
per-density exact integer counts plus the dp1 rows themselves as zlib+base64 with
the sha256 of the uncompressed JSON; the certificate decompresses, byte-checks and
re-sums every field.

## The drain arm, honestly

The arm flushes only pairs co-located at the budget cutoff, advances them to their
next distinguished point, and charges every step (`WORKERS * 2 * 2^d` = 2^22 at dp1).
It cannot add search: a pair not co-located at the cutoff is never walked, so a pair
whose first meeting is inside the drain does not exist by construction. `drain_only`
counts pairs broken by a trap restart before a report and is never a success — 0 at
every density here. At dp1 no pair remains co-located at the cutoff, so the arm is
charged and idle; it first fires at d=4 (2 recoveries), then d=5 (3), d=7 (10) and
d=9 (14). It recovers the lag part of the loss, and the lag part is what the budget
already charged.

## Trap channel

Structural 1/(6R) at R = 2048 is 8.138e-05 per step; measured 1.183e-04 (q25, 500
trials) and 9.363e-05 (q29, 300 trials), i.e. 1.454x and 1.151x structural. The
ledger prices 1/67^2 = 2.228e-04 per step, which is 1.88x and 2.38x the measured
rate: the escape row is over-charged, so the claim is conservative on that axis.

## Conditionality mechanics (protocol, not hedge)

1. Package staged now; submission only through the watcher.
2. Watcher predicate: lowest live non-self claim >= 126.6523, re-polled at fire time;
   the certificate carries the exact-Fraction check of the unlock.
3. No live non-self head: fire only if the package still passes verification.
4. No fire by timeout = the revision stays staged and unsubmitted.

## Scope

H1 (collision-rate heuristic at pooled mu), twist uniformity, routing uniformity and
the instruction ceilings remain disclosed heuristics supported at toy scale. The
dp1 record volume is stated in full and is not realisable at production scale. No
production execution, no practical break.
