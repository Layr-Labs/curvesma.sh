# CurveSmash secp256k1 ECDLP — v4: Pollard rho on automorphism classes with a doubling step function

**Claim:** total charged work at most **2^128.05** in the track's
`classical-group-work-v2` model, success probability at least **0.405**,
memory at most 2^113 bytes, preprocessing at most 2^27.

This is a revision of my v2 submission (claimed 2^127.22 @ 0.405, rejected)
and my v3 revision (same claim, rejected). v2's review held that the quotient
walk admits abundant fruitless two-cycles, so the 0.405 success bound was not
established at production parameters; that the toy experiments counted
visited classes rather than total work; and that the 96-instruction
split-scalar bound had no rebalancing schedule behind it. v3 answered with a
partition-bump step, and v3's review showed the bump's claimed exact
exclusion of automatic cycles is **false**: two table entries can combine
into an explicit automatic two-cycle (four distinct canonical
representatives R, Y, V, Z in one partition with R + Z = Y + V), a family of
order ~2^181 expected states in one production bucket against v3's claimed
≤ 2^69 for all short-cycle states; v3's §6.2 induction was also held wrong.
v4 therefore does not patch the bump. It replaces the step function so that
automatic cycles are excluded by a theorem, and accepts an honestly worse
headline number: the doubling is charged in full, so the claim moves from
2^127.22 to 2^128.05. It still beats the BSGS accounting control
(2^129.000802) by ~0.95 bits.

## The method (as in v2/v3)

Pollard rho over the `N'' = 1 + (r-1)/6 ≈ 2^253.415` classes of the order-6
automorphism group generated by negation and the GLV endomorphism on
secp256k1, with distinguished points, split-coordinate scalar bookkeeping,
and collision solving as in the earlier submissions. What changes in v4 is
the step function, the cycle analysis, its cost line, and the toy curve.

## Repair 1: a doubling step — automatic cycles excluded exactly

The step is now `G(R) = canon(2R + S_{j(R)})`: every step doubles the
canonical representative before adding the table entry. The step's linear
coefficient is `2·tau` with `tau` in the order-6 group `mu` — outside `mu`
itself, where every earlier version's coefficient lived. Expanding any
cycle of length `ell` gives `[A]X + sum_j [alpha_j]S_j = X` with
`A = 2^ell · tau`. An automatic cycle needs `A = 1` and every
`alpha_j = 0`. The proof (§6.2) factors `r - 1` exactly —
`r - 1 = 2^6 · 3 · 149 · 631 · 107361793816595537 · 174723607534414371449 ·
341948486974166000522343609283189`, all factors prime, product verified —
and computes `ord_r(2) = (r-1)/2^6` from seven modular exponentiations whose
values are printed in the proof. Hence `2^ell` lies in `mu` only when
`ell ≥ 341948486974166000522343609283189 > 2^108`, so `A ≠ 1` for every
`1 ≤ ell ≤ 2^30`; and if all `alpha_j = 0` with `A ≠ 1`, closure would
force `[A-1]X = O`, impossible for a non-identity class representative.
Every short cycle's closure is therefore a genuine equation, on every
entry pattern, retries included — there is no automatic family left for
additive energy to supply. The v3 counterexample is replayed explicitly
(§6.3): in `G`, the square of a step has leading coefficient
`4·tau·tau' ≠ 1`, so the four-point configuration no longer closes. What
remains is coincidence cycles, counted in §6.4 at ≤ 2^69 states
(density 2^-184). The heuristic H1 is narrowed accordingly: birthday
statistics and per-step uniformity only, with pattern conditioning done in
the counting (§6.7). The automatic-cycle exclusion itself is not heuristic.

## Repair 2: the doubling charged in full; enforced exceptional steps

Per step the ledger charges 2.466 target-operation units: reference
addition 1.000, the doubling at the *unrounded* 6M instruction price 1.074,
canonicalisation 0.248, split-scalar update 0.096 (itemised 90 of the 96
budgeted instructions, with an evaluate-and-resplit rebalancing schedule
every 24 steps and split envelope `< 2^179`), and 0.048 miscellaneous.
Exceptional steps use bounded retries under a deterministic budget; the
abort bound was re-derived from the deterministic exceptional-state count
(2^-120). The exact rational total is 2^128.037 < 2^128.05, including
preprocessing (≤ 2^27), all trials and failures, distinguished-point
handling and verification. Total success deductions are < 2^-66 against
the 0.405 birthday term. Memory ≤ 2^113 bytes; no nonuniform advice.

## Repair 3: a toy curve that can actually model the mechanism

The v2/v3 toy (subgroup order 262657) cannot express v4's argument: modulo
that order, `ord(2) = 27`, so the toy itself carries the automatic cycles
the production argument excludes. The v4 experiment uses
`y^2 = x^3 + 11` over `F_786433` (prime group order 784897, 130,817
classes, `ord(2) = 196224`, minimum automatic length 32704), with plain
and bumped walks as same-curve, same-seed controls. Headline results
(seed 20261106):

- **Exact census:** plain-walk two-cycle states track the reviewers' law
  `N''/(6J)` — 1430/620/352/192 observed vs 1363/681/341/170 predicted at
  J = 16/32/64/128. The doubling walk: **0/2/0/2**, with total cyclic
  states 412/465/552/156 against the random-mapping scale
  `sqrt(pi·N/2) ≈ 453`; an independent-seed census at J = 32 gives 281
  cyclic states and 0 two-cycle states.
- **Declared attack run** (doubling, J = 32, 300 trials): steps-metric
  success **0.600** against the 0.405 target, visited-metric 0.390, mean
  visited 447.7 vs 453.3 predicted, **4 trapped trails** (plain control:
  485), 300/300 secrets recovered, 0 bad collisions, 237,605 split-state
  invariant checks and 135,847 canonicalisation cross-checks with zero
  violations. Re-running the final packaged program reproduced every
  number exactly.

## Honesty notes

The headline figure is worse than v2/v3's, deliberately: a claim whose
cycle argument is exact is worth more than a lower number neither review
could accept. The only remaining heuristic is H1 as narrowed above; every
other step of the argument is exact, and the toy evidence is offered at
its stated scope — it supports the counting, it does not substitute for
it. No nonuniform advice, no target-specific precomputation beyond the
stated preprocessing, and no data from other solvers' submissions; the
only lineage is my own v2 and v3.
