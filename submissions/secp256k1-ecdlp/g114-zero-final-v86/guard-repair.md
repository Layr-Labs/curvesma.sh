# Cursor persistence and guarded hop-2 slots

This version changes the group-final fin routine by storing the clamped logical final cursor back into the sender's RC table, as well as sending it to E2. The extra STR is charged on every fin destination at both hops and every group: FIN_DEST is15 rather than14 and FINAL is1054 rather than1053. Hop-1 slots and sender cursors are reinitialized each group; hop-2 starts the next group at its saved logical cursor. All group transitions occur only after the existing completion/barrier protocol.

Each hop-2 slot has cap_p logical records followed by k=256 physical guard records. Its physical stride is32*(cap_p+k), while its logical end remains base+32*cap_p. A full-width block is sent only when its start c is below that logical end. Its last write is at c+32*k-8, which is at most logical_end+32*(k-1)-8, strictly inside its guard. fin's partial block is delivered at the same full width but only L records are valid, and its RC/E2 are clamped to min(c+32*L,end). A full block that straddles the logical end is similarly clipped at E2; subsequent sends are dropped and fin clamps RC. A group consumes records only up to E2. After the barrier, the next group begins at that E2 and can overwrite only the stale suffix beyond it, preserving all earlier valid records. Thus delivery order within a group and fin completion remain essential. Hash walks process only logical addresses, never guard data; the next-array mapping reserves a word per physical slot position, including holes between logical regions.

R1 begins at2^52 and has 9214364837600034816 bytes. R2 begins at9218868437227405312 and its guarded end is18433796224780861440, below2^64 by12947848928690176 bytes. Within R1, per-group hop-1 slots,2^57 head words and the next array covering SIDE*(cap_p+k) physical positions fit. These exact integer checks are in certificate.py; the peak-memory ceiling remains2^135.9989. The stored-next mapping is NEXTBASE+((A-R2BASE)>>2), equivalently (A>>2)+C; slot and guard addresses are aligned by32. Guard bytes are memory, not free work or payload: existing full-width delivery is charged, including writes into guard. No extra guard initialization is needed because hash reads stop at E2. The bounded phase-control reserve covers setup of the revised public stride; all per-destination fin writes are directly in the E and worst-case ledgers.
