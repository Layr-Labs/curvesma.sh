**Reviewer's guide.**

2. Charged: a window is a bounded queue in shared memory; its addressing (one AND a record), transfers (the owner's store, the reader's load), sweeps (of empty windows too), reuse (the owner's reset) and synchronisation (barrier waits) are instructions of shipped kernels, counted by executing them (3.1, 5). 5. The run executes every transport kernel on machine.py's memory model; what it does not show: 5.1 item 3, section 8.

## 1. Overview
On secp256k1 (y^2=x^3+7 over F_p, p=2^256-2^32-977, prime order r, phi(x,y)=(beta x, y)=[lambda](x,y)), Phase 0 mines 8 points X_(p,0..7) in 4 pairs (0<delta_A_q=A_(p,2q+1)-A_(p,2q)<2^63, A=y^2 mod p) per pool across N_POOLS=2^14 GF(1024) pools, of which Phases 1-5 use MX=6 (X_(p,0..5), 3 pairs) to label 6 leaves x(phi^t(X) +- Y) (t=0,1,2) from 1/D (D=y_Y^2-y_X^2) via (Ew-b)+beta^t(Fw-a)+beta^(2t) Gw, with E, F, G held as K' Eisenstein pairs (3.3), over 2^66 generators, 2^65 relays, 2^82 homes (HB=50) and 2^46 Y-relays.
## 2. Algorithm, Phase 0, and Armv9.6-A VMSAv9-128 two-55-bit-range machine
Roles (scalar AArch64; C=1000, COST_MODEL.md, classical-group-work-v2.json). Generator (g1,g2), g1<2^25, g2<2^41 (2^66 nodes; 2^56 clusters, also sub-rings, of M_G=1024): node 1024 c+m with g1=(c mod 2^15) << 10 | m, g2=c >> 15. It is the virtual node of cluster c (Phase 0, X set-up, chain coins, requests) and the worker of sub-ring sigma=(s << 15)|(c mod 2^15) with slice s=g2 (fwd, fe_inv, bwd, keys); member 0 of a cluster is its producer (yround, yprod) and member 0 of a sub-ring holds its Y frames (ym0). Relay (g1,v), v<2^40 (2^65): rsw, rqsw, rswe. Home (v,e), e<2^42 (2^82): hsw into R2 (CAP_H=1,313,229,200,424,960 records; section 4), hreq, hswe, hhclr, resolve. Y-relay (c mod 2^15, p1, s1), p1<2^17, s1<2^14 (2^46): yrel.

Machine (Arm DDI 0487 M.d, VMSAv9-128): a multiprocessor whose nodes share only the windows of premise SW; each node is an Armv9.6-A processor at EL1 with its own translation regime and physical address map, without EL2 or EL3: TCR2_EL1.D128=1, TCR_EL1.T0SZ=T1SZ=9, TG0=0b01 and TG1=0b11 (64-KiB granules), EPD0=EPD1=TBI0=TBI1=0, IPS=0b111 (56-bit PA), SCTLR_EL1.M=C=1 after MMU_ON. With two VA ranges VMSAv9-128 allows at most 55-bit VAs: VA bit[55] selects TTBR0_EL1 for [0, 2^55) or TTBR1_EL1 for [2^64-2^55, 2^64); a64.py `va55` rejects data accesses outside both. The walk has a level 0 of 8 entries (VA[54:52]) and three levels of 4,096 (VA[51:40], [39:28], [27:16]); level-2 Blocks map 256-MiB interiors and level-3 Pages their edges, as Normal Write-Back Cacheable Inner Shareable memory, EL1 read/write, or read only where the node is a window's reader. Per node, memory+table PA PT[k][3]<2^56 B (tables: NODE_INIT in 5.1; MMU_ON=64; c39-c40); a translated load or store adds no instruction. All memory is Normal, there is no Device memory: LD64B/ST64B use Normal memory by FEAT_LS64WB, as do the LSE atomics (LDADD on cursors, STADD, SWP), LDAXR/WFE and DC ZVA (BS=9, 2048 B). No kernel executes a DMB: order between nodes is STLR/LDAXR on arrival and release words (premise SW). FEAT_CMPBR register forms CBNE, CBHI, CBHS (+-1 KiB reach, flags kept; a64.py decodes and range-checks them) are keys' index tests, EMIT's full test and route.s's full tests, empty tests and loop bounds.

Windows and sweeps. A record window is 8 KiB of its owner's memory: word 0 is the cursor (an address in the owner's VA) and 255 slots of 32 B, [K2,K3,w2,w3], lie at offsets 32 to 8,160. The owner's reset (wreset) stores base+8,160 into the cursor; a store is LDADD of -32 on the cursor, which returns the slot, and CBNE against the base: in a full window the owner stores the base back and drops the record (success.md 6.4, d_h1, d_h2). A reader loads the cursor and then only slots above the value it loaded; it never stores to a window, the cursor included: every reset is the owner's. Hop 1: a worker owns 2^40 windows, window v at VA 8192 v for relay digit v=L(v*) >> 24 (3.2); EMIT forms the address by `and x0,x1,Lv,lsr #11`, x1=(2^40-1) << 13 (an immediate), and nothing else chooses the destination. Relay (g1,v) reads window v of its 2^41 generators (g1,*) and owns 2^42 out-windows, window e at VA 8192 e for home digit e=K2[41:0] (`and x0,x10,K2,lsl #13`; x10=(2^42-1) << 13 and the table base x25 below are arguments the relay's driver sets); home (v,e) reads out-window e of its 2^25 relays (*,v). rsw walks the relay's in-windows in address order: it loads the cursor (an owner address; all in-windows of a relay have the owner base 8192 v), loads a code address from the jump table at JT+8(cursor-8192 v) (`ldr x7,[x25,x5,lsl #3]`: 256 fills at a stride of 256 B, and at JT+64 the exit, selected by the sentinel word 8192 v+8 that follows the last in-window) and branches (BR) into an unrolled chain that copies record pairs (LD64B) to their out-windows by the store above. hsw does the same at a home, appending each pair as one 64-B line to the record store R2 (ST64B) and inserting each record into the hash chains (HCHGI); an odd count leaves a pad slot in R2, never chained, counted against R2's capacity (PADS_H, d_p2); a home whose R2 fill is within one sweep (2^25 windows of 256 slots) of its limit skips the sweep after one compare, and its records are lost (d_p2). A hop-1 wave is T1=849,000 rounds of leaf work (a group's last may be shorter): workers reset their windows before each wave, relays sweep after it, homes sweep after every W2=3 waves and at a group's end, and a relay resets its out-windows after each home sweep. Sweeps of empty windows are executed and counted (5).

Group end. While sweeping, a home lists candidates, pairs of records with equal K3 and d4 (up to CAND_CAP=4,164,000,000; later ones are not listed). At a group's end hreq writes one request a record of each new candidate, [w2, w3, tag, g2], into the home's H -> R cell of relay (g1,v), g1=w2[27:3], g2=w3[41:1]; the tag is the address of the reply slot in the home's own memory. A cell is 128 B, a cursor word and 3 slots of 32 B, stored like a window; a full cell drops the request (d_rqh). A home with no new candidate does not call hreq: its driver loads the list fill word and compares it with the list's base (two instructions of PH_STEP, 5.1). Relays sweep their 2^42 H -> R cells (rqsw) and store each request as [w2, w3, tag, e] into their R -> G cell of generator g2 (rqf; a full cell: d_rqg). Generators sweep their 2^40 R -> G cells (gqsw, gqf), reset their hop-1 windows, serve each request (REQ_SERVE, 5.1) and store a 10-slot reply (a line with e and the tag, 256 B of payload) into hop-1 window v (grpl). Relays reset their out-windows, find the non-empty in-windows (rswe) and move each reply to out-window e (rrf); homes find theirs (hswe) and store each reply at its tag (hrf), the home's own value carried unchanged by rqf, gqf, grpl and rrf. The reply path has no full test and needs none: a window reset before the reply wave receives at most 3 replies of 10 slots, since a cell holds 3 requests (c1). A dropped request leaves its reply slot empty (hreq stores 0 there) and its candidate unresolved; RESOLVE (5.1) acts on complete pairs. The stop bit travels as barrier payload.

Y path. In a round the producer (member 0 of cluster c') computes its L_CHAIN Ys (yround) and stores each, a slice word and the 448-B record, as a 512-B slot of its producer window s >> 27 (yprod; 2^14 windows of 2^30 B; no full test: a round stores L_CHAIN slots after the producer's reset, c41). Y-relay (c' mod 2^15, p1, s1) reads window s1 of its 2^24 producers (c' >> 39=p1) and moves each record to its out-window s mod 2^27 (yrel; 65,535 slots of 512 B; a full window drops the record: d_y). Member 0 of sub-ring (s, c' mod 2^15) reads that out-window of its 2^17 Y-relays and copies the records into its frame area YQ (ym0), stopping at B_CAP=2^26-1 frames (d_drop). Each of the 1,024 members then loads member 0's frame cursor (rdcur) and runs fwd and bwd on the frames in place, both through the read-only window YA (member 0 through an alias of its own YQ). No kernel stores into a frame after ym0 (3.1).

Barriers. Every node owns an arrival cell, read by its tree parent, and a release cell, read by its children (4 KiB each: the arrival or release word alone in the first 2-KiB granule; in the second, two payload words and, in a tree-1 release cell, the coin line at +2,112). bleaf: a node stores its payload and its arrival word (STLR), waits until its parent's release word shows the barrier's number (LDAXR, CMP, B.EQ, WFE, B) and loads the release payload. bpar: a parent waits likewise for each child's arrival word and ORs the payloads; brel stores the release payload and word (STLR). Tree 0 holds all nodes at 9-digit positions in base 1,024: a position's parent has its lowest non-zero digit cleared and its level is its number of trailing zero digits, so a node of level z has up to 1,023 z children. Generators take every position of level >= 2 and, the rest, positions of level 1 (c62), so a generator's parent is a generator. Tree 1 (7 digits, generator g at position g) holds generators and Y-relays for a round's three tier barriers (after yprod, yrel, ym0). There member 0 of a cluster is the parent of its members: before its brel of the round's first tier barrier it copies the round's coin pair, a 64-B line, from its CN slot into its release cell, and each member copies it into its own CN slot after its bleaf (cpair; for REQ_SERVE, see Phases 1-5). Every step of this section ends in a barrier, and in a step a node reads only what others stored in earlier steps: the order of premise SW. After Phase 0 the ledger counts 333,816 global barriers (allowance 335,872): 2 a hop-1 wave, 1 a home sweep, 6 a group end (the run uses 5), 9 at start and end; Phase 0 has NB0=NGM(2 WGM+7)+8=1,072 more in E_MINE (the same counts a mining group; 8 for the start, the five tree passes and hhclr with the R2 restart), each a full BARRIER; P0_TREE charges six tree waves' waits once more (5.1).

1. *Memory model (machine.py, a64.py).* a64.py executes and counts the instructions of one kernel call on one node's memory; machine.py is that memory for every node of the run, at production addresses and field widths. ## 8. Limitations
Limitations. (1) SW is a premise about the system's memory map, which the Arm architecture leaves to the system: the stores into windows and loads from them, the resets, sweeps and barrier waits are charged, but that one memory word is reachable from two nodes (a sub-ring's frames from its 1,024 members, a release cell from up to 9,207 children, a down-list from up to 8,184), and the wiring of the windows counted in the premise table, are assumed and uncharged, and no physical fabric is specified. With two-party windows only (one writer, one reader: each Y record stored once more for each of the 1,023 other members, 30 instructions a copy; one release cell, coin line and down-list copy a child) the claim would be 123.2820, at most 0.0064 bits above the claim: the frame copies are the difference, and the per-child stores fit BARRIER, TIERB, BATCH_SETUP and P0_TREE (c57). (2) FEAT_CMPBR has no shipping implementation, and DCZID_EL0.BS=9 is IMPLEMENTATION DEFINED: with 64-byte DC ZVA blocks a home zeroing pass (ledger row 10) of the same form takes 4,098 instructions, not 130, and the claim would be 123.3322 (c56). (3) MW is assumed: the bound on the passes of a wait loop (5.1 item 2) holds under it and is not derived from an architecture rule. (4) The claim is an expectation; the every-run ceiling is nearly 50 bits above it, because home walks, listings and resolves are bounded only by CAP_H and CAND_CAP. (5) The experiment runs the kernels at small scale on a memory model in which nodes run one at a time: no interleaving inside a kernel call is modelled, nor the timing of a store against a concurrent load. For the order between nodes the run offers the executed barrier kernels, the comparison of two processor orders and its controls; that a window is not read while it is written rests on the barriers. The planted collision is degenerate (one leaf point under two record ids, not two different points with one label) and the toy does not mine (5.1 item 3); the meter skip of 3.4 is not exercised. Six files ship compressed (secp_fe.s, leafm.s, a64.py, leafx.py, cases.py, toy.py; section 7): for a reader of the package as shipped, what 3.1 says of the instructions inside leafm.s's macros and secp_fe.s's kernels rests on the counts the run measures by executing them, not on a readable source. Production-scale bounds are analytic (certificate.py). (6) HH zeroing beyond one executed zero16384 pass a home is analytic (5.1 cold start). (7) Work counted by itemization, with no kernel executed for it in the run (5.1): REQ_SERVE, RESOLVE and XSETUP, which Python performs on words loaded from the acting node's memory (the point REQ_SERVE returns in Phase 0 is a test input); a node's driver between barriers (PH_STEP); node set-up, including the jump tables, sentinel words and control words that the harness writes and the zero in each arrival and release word; the Phase-0 tree passes at production sizes, whose kernels are run on small lists only. (8) Lemma J rests on a cited theorem (Kohel-Shparlinski, inequality (3); Weil's bound through Bombieri): without it the hop-1 window loads are not bounded and the claim does not stand. The run evaluates the lemma's exponential sums on a 13-bit toy curve only.
