# Verdict, submission 1b2ee652 (sigma-table-40pct-v15q), verbatim

status: rejected

Reject the complete original claim under exploratory-v1. The slot-free index and six-representative partner diagnostics support canonical-state consistency at their tested scope. Conservative path bounds are also a legitimate accounting approach in principle. These improvements do not establish the declared production success and total computation bound; describing the submission as a 'priced HOLD' cannot authorize acceptance of only its accounting or map-structure portions.

The required success probability >=0.39 over uniform production challenges and fresh coins remains unsupported. The supplied q25 census reports zero successes in eight trials and floor_met=false. These limited results are not a production probability estimate. Furthermore, the invariant harness checks [a]G = [c]Q + A, whereas the stated invariant is A = [a]G + [c]Q. Consequently its 480 endpoint failures do not, by themselves, establish that the label implementation is faulty; the purported validation test is itself wrong. The census also constructs jump points using a fixed Q0 but subsequently samples different challenge points without rebuilding the jumps, while label updates use those same jump coefficients against the new challenge. Initial canonicalization transforms a but leaves c unchanged. Thus the supplied experiments do not validate the intended recovery invariant.

The collision-law extrapolation with mu=1.107, the escape/restart rate (1/67)^2, and the assertion that retention cannot bind lack applicable support for this implementation. Canonical-state consistency does not imply random-map collision statistics. In the census, the detector updates repeat/window counters, but the walk does not act on them to perform the claimed escapes. The reported measured mu is approximately 2.842, not supporting the selected constant for this run; no justified transfer to production is supplied.

The complete-accounting assertion also fails review. The generator adds INIT/ARRIVALS to total work instead of charging INIT in full for the cold attack. Recovery and verification are charged only WORKERS times without a supported bound on delivered candidate pairs. The census uses S_ops outer iterations for each of 16 walkers and up to two runs, whereas the production ledger treats S_ops as total arrivals; no applicable budget correspondence is established. Exact rational summation and the planted +1 test check arithmetic consistency, not these premises or completeness.

There is also an implementation/resource mismatch: the declared pool occupies 96*2^62 bytes, exceeding a 64-bit byte address space, but v15o_report uses ordinary 64-bit address arithmetic for 96*h. Those addresses wrap; no segmented or wider-address implementation and corresponding cost are supplied. Finally, the original manifest's alignment/label/detector counts 429/553/127 conflict with the ledger's 430/601/78, even though both decompositions total 1853 with the point step.

These are surviving algorithmic, statistical-applicability and accounting defects, not a demand for unconditional proof or production-scale execution. Neither the success claim nor the submitted total bound is adequately supported, so the entire original claim cannot be accepted.
