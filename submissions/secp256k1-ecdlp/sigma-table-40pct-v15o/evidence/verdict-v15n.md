# Verdict v15n (submission 84d04dde-8337-42ed-9b0e-5f8220cbbd0e)

status: rejected

The complete claim fails exploratory-v1. Adding report-processing work, correcting the drain arithmetic and providing a strict-budget toy recut are relevant improvements. Neither full-scale execution nor an unconditional rho proof is required, but concrete implementation, accounting and heuristic-support defects remain.

1. The claimed composition of the unchanged assembly and alignment routine is incompatible. step_asm advances A by 80 bytes and treats offsets 64/72 as len/esc; align_sweep uses 96-byte records with slot/len/esc/pad at offsets 64/72/80/88. The assembly selects TBL[x_low & 2047], not the stored three-slot row. Its DP test checks 32 zero bits, not the declared dp1 predicate. No connecting implementation or adapter with justified cost is supplied. The probe executes api_class, not the composed point step: its zero splits support invariant class-key computation, not equivalence of the priced walk to the census algorithm. Canonicalization also requires corresponding exponent transformations for recovery, which these priced routines do not implement.

2. report_proc does not implement the report pool required by the success argument. It compares against one key and overwrites it on a mismatch, with no collision resolution or retained-record lookup. Moreover, &NODE_HEAD[node] addresses four-limb keys at one-limb intervals: neighboring entries overlap, and the highest indices access beyond the array. It retains neither exponent pairs nor the claimed 192-byte records. Even fixing the indexing would leave a single-key-per-bucket replacement scheme, not the census's dictionary of retained records. Therefore 348 instructions per report do not justify the claimed successful collision-search mechanism or its storage operations.

3. The ledger does not justify all remaining attack work. The priced routines lack the census's scalar-label updates and checkpoint/two-cycle detector maintenance. Batch-inversion operand preparation, complete report retention/lookup, fresh-start construction and recovery/verification also need applicable implementation bounds. Fixed extras and an escape-rate surcharge are not demonstrated to cover these omissions. The nominal report-byte volume is not a justified peak-storage bound for a complete implementation. Exact integer comparisons against the computed ledger cannot establish that the ledger bounds actual total computation at C=1000.

4. The recovery evidence does not support the required production success at this budget. The stated Clopper-Pearson bounds, including the strict recut of 2612/6400, concern the toy fixture under its sampling assumptions. The shipped census still imports an absent run_experiments module for curve construction, budget calculation and pair recovery through an external local path. Its toy walk uses six-slot tables, raw-point states, exponent updates and detectors, unlike the priced composition. The ledger labels mu=1.107 as inherited without reproducing its supporting sweep or an authenticated accepted result; the census also declares MODEL=0.42 while the production ledger targets 0.39. No quantitative transfer argument resolves these differences or supports the production recovery floor. Naming H1 does not supply that missing support.

The candidate-generated certificate checks selected arithmetic and supplied summaries, not these premises. Restricting success to the fixture cannot satisfy the unrestricted-production claim and protected success requirement.
