# STNFS v14: the window-sieved descent, lean record routing and a 123-bit ledger

Track: bls12-381-fixed-g2-invert-g1. Fixed reference C=20,000 scalar AArch64
instructions per dense Fp12 multiplication. No auxiliary powers, smaller target,
free preprocessing, future-target amortization or accepted prior results.

## Claim

The specified classical attack returns P with e(P,Q)=z for the exact supplied
nonidentity Q, with success >=0.39 over uniform full-production challenges and
fresh coins, under the supported exploratory heuristics disclosed here, in
DESCENT.md and in STORAGE.md. The modelled success is >=0.794. An instruction
meter limits total work, including every failure, restart, transfer and output,
to **2^123 reference units**; the submitted rational score is **123/1**. The
ledger below totals 2^122.637 units, leaving 0.363 bits of reserve. Memory is
reported separately (<=2^81 bytes of distributed aggregate storage; every
individual allocation bounded and explicitly addressed). Identity z returns
infinity. No unconditional production-success proof or full-size run is claimed.

This is a new claim and algorithm relative to the rejected v13: the descent is a
different node algorithm (DESCENT.md), the record routing is a different protocol
(STORAGE.md), the linear-algebra layout uses 16 blocks at 88 instructions/entry,
and the v13 prose/code parameter conflicts (CF128/CF64, 96/128 LA entries, cap
121/122, the +160/+28 ECM operand offsets and the -32-bit survivor slack) are
withdrawn and replaced by one parameter set shared by code, prose and manifest.

## 7. Individual logarithms: the window-sieved descent

DESCENT.md specifies the complete node algorithm. Summary: every node,
including the boot (one p^6-determinant lattice, g-side envelope 2800 bits,
lB1 = 180, k = 2), is a bucket-sieved special-q lattice node: LLL-reduced basis,
certified c-space envelope screen along the shortest-basis direction (the
embedding-bound machinery with interval a and b; envelopes 72c+76 and
12c+6log2|u|+17; measured retained fraction 0.999), a bucket sieve over primes
p in [2^8, 2^69] with per-(prime, root pair) hit-lattice walks
(PAIR_SETUP 2^24.8, SIEVE_CELL 2^21.64 kernel-measured), a threshold on
the residual mass with a bounded child window (k in {2,3,4,6} primes above
2^69, residual slack 96 bits), ECM on survivors' residuals (the residual, not
the full norm, is factored: 2^(k n + 96) bits at level ceil(n/3.32) digits), and
conditional child means given the window bound. Survivors are processed until
4 x MIN_RELS = 32 relations are found and verified. The no-sieve ECM-per-trial
control is retained at every bin (the honest full schedule: after it, every
remaining factor exceeds 2^n, so the trial is decided); the recurrence
(2-bit bins, exact k and S grids, target grid step 2 below bin 150 and 4 above,
the coarser grid overestimating by +0.05 bits) gives two-log work
**2^105.91 units** ([7], at the measured walk anchor; the production
leaves-per-hit extrapolation and both-end sensitivities are in DESCENT.md; the
v14 Poisson-CDF window model that ignored the sub-2^69 smoothness cost is
withdrawn, per the review).

The node mechanics are validated by the toy differential [8] (sampled in the
sandbox; the exhaustive research runs are summarised in
stats14.json): the congruence functionals w.c = 0 mod p agree exactly
with ideal membership (both sides, 0 mismatches), the score is the distinct
sieved-prime mass, the threshold has no false negatives, and survivors' credited
children are exactly their residual window primes (no false credits). The
production norm studies ([11], DESCENT.md) bound the envelopes.

H5 (the expectation allowance of 128 for backtracking, repeats, correlated
children, ECM variety, rank dependencies and implementation overheads) gives
expected two-log work < 2^112.91 units. The descent meter caps both logs at
**2^116 units**: Markov gives P(cap exceeded) <= 2^-3.09, so modelled success is
at least 0.9 (1 - 2^-3.09) = **0.794** (>= 0.39). The success bound rests on the
mass-weighted production leaves-per-hit being at most about 400; the 254 anchor
at the mass-weighted prime supports this, the worst measured end (1663) would
not, and that dependence is disclosed here rather than hidden. The finite-tree viability
beyond MIN_RELS = 8 and the pads remains the disclosed heuristic limitation.
