Rejected under exploratory-v1. The fixed-Q reduction is sound: nondegeneracy makes g=e(P0,Q) a generator, and the collision equation recovers x when its denominator is nonzero. Charging restarts/replay and investigating abelian cancellations are useful improvements. Nevertheless, the complete original claim has surviving material defects.

1. The asserted exact record/step identity is false. The ledger gives records times mean trail length as 71551125967113754319928145100077531136, not the declared S=71551125138390883155967982189214756238. More importantly, multiplying a record count by an assumed geometric mean does not establish executed work. Actual trail lengths fluctuate, and truncation changes their mean. The certificate checks only a rounding inequality. Metering can enforce the work cap, but the submission does not establish its stated success probability before exhaustion using the actual stochastic protocol; expected replay and trap charges alone do not supply that argument.

2. The success extrapolation is unsupported. The 763/768 result concerns N=43420, R=128, depth=2, with a different trail budget. It does not establish that 0.9863602375 is a multiplicative retention factor for the production birthday model. Twelve trials share each target/table fixture, so the hypotheses for an exact pooled binomial Clopper-Pearson guarantee over fresh fixtures are also unestablished. The guard evidence likewise explicitly uses depth 2, despite the note describing it as a depth-5 audit. Solving S backwards from an assumed retention factor cannot validate that factor or certify the resulting 0.390100 production floor.

3. The trap-rate analysis is not exact as declared. Its probability calculation explicitly assumes uniform slot/kappa behavior under H4, and enumeration stops at length 16 without a supplied bound for the omitted tail. There is also a combinatorial overcount: under its independent uniform-slot model, a restricted-growth pattern with m distinct slots has probability (R)_m/R^n. The script instead uses R^{-(n-m)} times m!, although restricted-growth words already remove slot relabelling. For abab this yields 2/R^2 instead of (1-1/R)/R^2 before branch/kappa factors. That overcount may be conservative, but it contradicts the claimed exact rate and does not bound omitted behavior. With a history-dependent guard, returning to the same group element also does not by itself prove recurrence of the complete walk state.

4. Essential current algorithmic support is missing. toy21.py imports Tower and field_params from an absent v20 package, including the unspecified keys12 routine. No authenticated prior result or supplied source fills this gap; references to an 'unchanged' construction are insufficient. The numerical instruction prices for key extraction and cyclotomic squaring are listed without the implementation-level derivation needed to validate the submitted tight total-work bound.

These are not demands for a production-sized execution or unconditional proof. The supplied experiments and arithmetic certificates do not sufficiently support the actual production algorithm, success extrapolation, exact protocol assertions, and total resource claim together. Consequently the original claim at time_log2=125.7908 cannot be accepted.
