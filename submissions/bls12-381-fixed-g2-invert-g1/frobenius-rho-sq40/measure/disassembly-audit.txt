3. Static vs dynamic reconciliation
-----------------------------------
Summing the composition of section 2 with each symbol body counted once gives
  144 + 3 x 2,860 + 3 x 88 + 15 x 36 + 90 = 9,618, plus the 1-instruction exported wrapper = 9,619.
The dynamic Callgrind count is 9,803 per call. The gap is 184 instructions (1.88%): one `ret` for
every executed call (181 executed call sites per squaring) plus the few instructions around them.
The static sum cannot see them because a symbol body is counted once while its `ret` executes per
call; and the two shared assembly entry points __mul_by_1_mont_384 and __redc_tail_mont_384 are
entered mid-body from several callers, so a per-symbol sum is a lower bound on their executed
share. The two figures therefore agree to within 1.9%, and the dynamic count - exact to the
instruction, identical at n = 1, n = 65 and all five slice offsets - is the number the ledger
charges. The static audit's role is to confirm the order of magnitude and the straight-line
property, not to set the price.

4. What the ledger charges, and why it is 9,798 rather than 9,803
------------------------------------------------------------------
results.tsv gives I(n=1) = 9,823 and I(n=65) = 637,215, so the marginal per call is
(I_65 - I_1)/64 = (637,215 - 9,823)/64 = 9,803.0, exactly, at every offset and every routine.
That marginal is the like-for-like counterpart of the cost model's "executed instructions,
including call and loop work" convention, which is why the same harness reproduces the model's
two reference counts exactly: blst_fp_mul = (24,915 - 403)/64 = 383.0 and
blst_fp12_mul = (1,494,761 - 23,017)/64 = 22,996.0.
The per-call marginal includes the harness's own loop and call control (5 instructions per
iteration: increment, compare, branch, call and one more), and the walk loop's control is
already charged in the step ledger's common rows (30 instructions of loop control, output
buffer and slot pointer), so the squaring row charges the routine's own body,
9,803 - 5 = 9,798, plus 52 for the alpha/beta doubling and nothing else. Charging the full
9,803 instead would raise the squaring step by 5 instructions (2.5e-4 units) and log2 W by
2.5e-4 bits, which moves no printed digit; the 10,098 budget is 9,798 + 300 and its counted
rows are 9,951, leaving 147 instructions of slack that already absorb the difference.
