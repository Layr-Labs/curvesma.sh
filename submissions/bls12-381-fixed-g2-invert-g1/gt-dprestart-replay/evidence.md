## 7. Experiments (`experiment.py`, standard library only)

* **Part A (BLS12-381):** GT membership; Frobenius formula = p-th power; 4-multiplication key
  formula = keys of the 12 explicit conjugates, all distinct; kappa picks one identical canonical
  element from every conjugate; p^6 inverts; ord_r(p) = 12; 1+p^4+p^8 = 0; r-1 factorisation;
  ord_r(2); the Granger-Scott formula equals y*y with 9 Fp2 squarings and 34 Fp2 additions.
* **Part C (BLS12-381, R = 16):** a real walk of 2,000 steps per rule in GT with the branch rule
  T mod 64 and the table index from the 4 bits above the 6-bit branch field (keys only on multiply
  steps, twisted table, squaring, 2-cycle escape; no distinguished-point predicate and no R = 1024
  table). Checks: every one of the first 150 steps lands in the class of y^ M_h or y^^2 as computed
  with explicit Frobenius; the exponent invariant y = g^alpha z^beta at the end; the squaring
  fraction (37/64 expected) over the branch-rule decisions with its binomial standard deviation and
  z-score, chi-square of the low 6 bits and of the next 4 bits of Re(c_0), and the distinct T values,
  the last step with a new T and the period of T over the last 64 steps. The declared run reads:
  stored rule 0.5824 over 1,997 decisions (1,163 squarings), 0.42 points above 37/64, binomial
  standard deviation 0.0111, z-score 0.38; chi-square 59.56 (63 dof) for the low 6 bits and 16.57
  (15 dof) for the next 4; 3 two-cycle escapes, 1,997 distinct T, the last new one at step 1,999, no
  period, 0 class-check failures in the first 150 steps and the exponent invariant. The integer rule reads 0.5877 (z-score 0.86); its key index differs from the stored rule in 755 of 834 multiply steps.
* **Part S (bounded sampler):** exact reduction on a small modulus, the R_MAX = 128 all-reject cap returning
  the residue 1 after 128 attempts, and 20,000 seeded draws in range; prints the fixed maxima (2,320 and
  296,960 instructions; 14.848 units), acceptance r/2^255 > 1/2, exhaustion 2^-128 per sample and the
  2^-71.99 exhaustion allowance over a run's below 2^56.0001 samples under the ideal-source model (no distributional bound on the
  ChaCha20 stream is claimed); part D asserts the samples' blocks below 2^64.
* **Part D (ledger and measurement record):** reads `measure/results.tsv` and the six raw Callgrind
  records, asserts the three blst marginals (383, 22,996, 9,803) and each raw record's total, charges the two
  straight-line kernels generated by `kernels/gen.py` (multiply 14,402, squaring 6,777; `kernels/check_kernels.py`
  regenerates both and checks a reduced sample against a big-integer reference), and recomputes the ledger for
  four price vectors (asserting the first two; the third is the parent's 226-per-step vector, reported), the cap and its integer inequality, the drift table, the
  matcher's worst case (54 x 2^55 + 57 levels), the replay bound and stopping allowance, the fresh starts after reports, the walk budget, threshold and every-run
  inequality on integers, the word widths, the report, meter and switch charges, the restart counter,
  preprocessing and the non-walk sum, and the sampler's fixed charge.

* **Part B (toy fields, q = 67, 139, 163, 211, 379):** the complete algorithm (keys, kappa trick, twisted

Hits are counted as in census 2 (printed T_c/sqrt N plus 0.00005 at most S(x)/sqrt N; the closest printed value is 6.8e-6 from a threshold).
The toy succeeds more often than the model's 0.4005 because at `dp_bits` 3 about one visit in eight (12.2 to 12.5%
measured) is a fresh start, and a fresh start meets an earlier class at rate 1/N instead of the iterates' (1 - sum q^2)/N.
With a fraction phi of fresh starts, the per-visit rate is [1 - (1 - phi)^2 sum q^2]/N. That gives a predicted mean
T_c/sqrt N of 1.453 to 1.457 and P[T_c <= S(0.4005)] of 0.435 to 0.437, against the measured 1.440 to 1.461 and
0.429 to 0.448: q = 67 is about 3.4 standard errors above its prediction and its mean about 2.7 below, a toy-scale
deviation in the direction of more collisions that this small, short-trail field shows and census 4 does not. In production phi is about 2^-72, so the law is H1's, which censuses 1 and 2 test. The restart
discipline only adds visits that collide at least as often.
