# Descent

descent16.py is the model and the shipped plan; descent_checks.py evaluates it (tree, recurrence, probes) and re-checks its inputs (laws, residues, pools, campaigns). (a, b) in O_h^2 is a vector in Z^12 (power basis, common12.py). All sizes are in bits; B = 2^56 is the factor-base bound.

## Lattices

Lattices are reduced by descent_witness.reduce: LLL (delta 0.99) under an integer block form Q(a, b) = round(2^24 sum_s |a(s)|^2) + round(2^24 lam sum_s |w(s)|^e |b(s)|^2), s over the six complex embeddings of K_h. Nodes use QN (lam 3/2, e 1/2), the boot QB (lam 2^62, e 1: |a|^2 balanced against u |w| |b|^2 on the g side). Regions: radius-R balls in reduced-basis coordinates, one cell per +- pair.

## Targets and boot

T becomes T' = T^e, e uniform in [1, r), log T = log T'/e mod r. The boot lattice {(a, b) in O_h^2 : a z_1 + b z_0 = 0 in F_p12} (T' = z_0 + z_1 x) has determinant p^6; its basis is certified (rows in the lattice, Euclidean Gram determinant p^12, size and Lovasz conditions under QB in exact rationals). The boot element has the single g-side norm N(a^2 - u w b^2). The boot enumerates the radius-4 ball, computes each norm and runs the ecm16.py schedule: a relation has every prime <= 2^260, <= 12 in (2^56, 2^132.50], <= 1 above. An exhausted ball draws a new exponent and certified lattice (2^36 each).

## Nodes

A node is a special-q ideal (l bits, side f/g; (q, y - s) and a root t of the side polynomial at s) with reduced lattice L_q (det q) and a radius-R ball. Every p in [2^8, 2^56] and every relative-degree-one root pair is sieved on the ball (dsieve16, KERNELS.md; 1100 per hit, 12 per cell); every cell gets a certified upper bound on both norms (outward-rounded embeddings, charged 2400). Cells with residual mass (log2 bound - score - log2 q on the special-q side) <= T = kA nA + kB n + 96 on both sides survive (exact norms, trial division, ECM). Certificate: the bound pass also sums ss_f ss_g at every cell's bounds (q removed); a node whose certified mean is below its bin's charged pr is discarded as a failure before cofactorisation, so every used node's own H1 yield is >= the charged one. A relation has every residual prime <= 2^n per side, <= kA in (2^56, 2^nA] and kB <= 1 in (2^nA, 2^n]; these window primes are the children, each charged at the most expensive bin at or below its window.

## Yield and survivors

Per cell, the two-window semismooth probability P = sum_(jA <= kA, jB <= kB) 1/(jA! jB!) int rho((m - sum t + alpha)/56) prod dt/t is a lower sum (exact weight int dt/t per mesh cell, rho at its left endpoint, mesh 96; cells crossing sum t = m dropped); alpha = (0, 3.4) (charged above the design's averages). Norm sizes m come from offset bins measured on this design: 61,440 exact norms of uniform ball points on 160 special-q lattices (10 sizes 56-200 bits, both sides, radii 8-128, 64 points per (ideal, radius); measure.py, seeds in the code): cf = log2 N_f - 48c, cg = log2 N_g - 12c - 6 log2 u, c = (log2 q + log2 cells)/12; means 20.44 / -8.64, maxima 88.3 / 10.5. cf sextile maxima are paired with the overall cg maximum and shifted up by the worst per-(ideal, radius) mean minus the pooled mean (+10 cf, +4 cg). Boot norms: 6,144 exact norms on 16 certified boot lattices of GT elements (radii 4, 6, 8; cg mean -185.74, p90 -180.78, p99 -177.78, max -173.93, each + 2, the worst per-target mean minus the pooled mean rounded up). For pr the sextiles are subdivided (24 cf-quantile cells with cg maxima per cell; boot deciles), cell maxima dominating their cells; charged pr = min(sub-cell mean, 4 x sextile pr) at nodes, min(sub-cell mean, 1.5 x) at the boot. mu = cells x P x UDUP x detection x retention (ECM detection 1 - 0.001 x expected factors). Survivors per cell use (T/56) rho((m - T)/56) per side at each bin's lowest norms. Plan radii stay inside the sampled range (R <= 128; boot R in 4, 6, 8).

## Factor-base ideals without a logarithm

Columns are tau-orbits of 4 ideals. At the descent input of 2^49.85 relations an ideal of norm q = 2^(56 t) on side s occurs in a relation with probability kappa_s(t)/q, kappa_s(t) = rho(u_s - t)/rho(u_s), u_s = mean norms 417/471 over 56; a column occurs Poisson(lambda), lambda = 2^49.85 x 4 kappa_s/q, and a column occurring <= 2x is missing. Each node either discards (relations with a missing ideal rejected, mu x exp(-missing); always at q <= 2^77) or descends (each missing Q is sieved in the lattice of Q q', q' a fresh ~2^20 ideal with known log, charged as the worst discarding node with q <= 2^77); the boot descends.

## Retention

A cell counts only if base-primitive (fraction prod (1 - N(P)^-2) >= 0.8794; other cells repeat a relation, h = 1) and not divisible by a relative-degree-one ideal above a base prime of degree >= 2 with p >= 2^8 (ordinary fractions 0.9513/0.9917). A counted cell is rejected only when one side's omitted factor-base mass X (whole valuation at p < 2^8, excess powers above) exceeds 96 - 1 - m/128. X is bounded by independent local laws (retain16.py; O_h = Z[y], so the primes above p are (p, h_i)): for a root of multiplicity mu of the side polynomial over the residue field F_q, P(v_P >= j and t reduces to it) <= min(1/q, mu q^-ceil(j/mu)) (Hensel; equality q^-j for mu = 1). Exact laws where the leading coefficient 36 or w vanish: at the prime above 2 (2 = P^3, N(P) = 4) v_P(F) = 6 exactly when b is in P (probability 1/5), else 0; at 3 (inert) v_3(F) = 2 when b is in 3 O_h (1/730); at P5 (w in P5) v(F) = 4 exactly when a/b reduces to 0 (1/6). Excess powers above 2^9 are bounded per dyadic block as in retain16.large_block_pdf. X_up has means 10.68 (f) and 6.72 (g) bits, P(X > 96) = 1.40e-9 and 4.20e-10 per cell. Relation-conditioned loss <= sum_(x > s) P_up(x) Pss_hi(m - c64(x)) / (Pss_lo(m) sum_(x <= s) P_up(x)) at the node's largest norms (retain16.loss): retention = (1 - loss_f - loss_g) x base-primitive x ordinary >= 0.8285 at every node (largest one-side loss 1.0e-03).

## Recurrence and success

Distinct relations per node are negative binomial (mean mu, shape 0.5 for windows of <= 1 prime, 1 otherwise; H5). phi(node) = max((1 + mu (1 - psi)/r)^-r + DISC, 0.02), psi = window sum of children's phi; node work = own sieve + 1/(1 - psi) x children's work, cofactorisation lazy (descent16.lazy_fraction). The plan requires mu >= 18, phi <= 0.05, psi <= 0.8: here min mu 30.4, max phi 0.0499, max psi 0.738. DISC = 0.01 charges discarded nodes: 492 sampled ideals at the bins' top edges (6 per node at the shipped entry's window and radius, 96 cells each; cert.py) have certified/charged pr >= 2^0.72 (log2 mean 2.92, sd 0.86), none below 1 (95% bound 0.0061 <= DISC). Unit duplicates: UDUP = 0.65; exact unit orbits (unit_orbit.py) give a distinct yield per cell over the charged pr of 0.725 at the boot (8 lattices x 24 cells; mean - 2 SE 0.686, least lattice 0.650) and 1.47 (top bin, 12 cells per side).

The shipped plan: 82 nodes (bins (56, 62) and 5-bit bins to 262, both sides) and the boot (n1 = 260, nA = 132.50, kA = 12, R = 4); all nodes sieve (R = 10..128); base nodes R = 80 (f, mu 1078) and 80 (g, mu 949). Boot: whole balls are charged (2^36 + cells x trial, trial 2^55.62), E[balls] <= (1 - psi^18)/(1 - psi) x (1/mu + BALL_PAIR), 2^27.36 balls per logarithm (H1: BALL_PAIR = 2^24); at most 18 relations are descended, failure psi^18 <= 4.4e-03 per logarithm.

Expected two-log work <= 2^93.684 units (unrounded 2^93.68307; one logarithm 2^106.971 instructions: boot own 2^106.64 + subtree 2^102.73 x retry 3.813). Expected special-q nodes for both logarithms 2^16.80; the run aborts before node 2^20 + 1 (P <= 2^-3.19, Markov); a repeated ideal among them has P <= 2^-12.60 (2^39 pairs x 2^6 over the ideals of the smallest bin). Largest node: R = 128, 4 bytes per cell pair: 2^85.42 bytes; scores <= 22252 < 2^16.

## Carried kernels

The node sieve, bound, LLL, norm, trial-division and ideal prices and the ECM cost formula are those of the BLS12-381 package's descent kernels: they act on rank-12 lattices over a degree-6 base field with 64-bit primes and are basis-blind; here the lattices have the same rank, primes are below 2^56, norms are shorter (f has degree 8) and balls are smaller (R <= 128 against 384), so each price is used inside its measured range. The norm bound multiplies 48 + 12 embedding factors where the measured kernel multiplies 72 + 12.
