### 8.1 The enumeration in integers (`pt_orb`, `pt_geo`, `pt_rows`, `pt_e3`, `pt_cands`)

An orbit is an LLL-reduced basis `b_0..b_11` of `Lambda_p`; `Q(x) = sum_k E_k`, `E_k = r_k (x_k - c_k)^2`, `r_k = d[k+1]/d[k]`, `c_k = -sum_{j>k} mu_{jk} x_j`. ### 8.3 Fallback orbits

## 10. KNAP: Boot-Root Children Priced by the Gate Knapsack (No New Premise)

**Setting.** A boot root (`boot = [185, 80, 11, 1]`, `ea = [3, 1225]`) is a kept boot-lattice vector that is *usable*; the miniature `joint_ea_mini_audit` (`boot_dp.py`) applies the test. **(G)** Its rough part `cof` (the product, with multiplicity, of its prime factors above `2^{x_EA}`, `x_EA = 62.9375`) is `<= 2^{tau}`, `tau = 1225` (the early abort of `ADAPTIVE.md` §1.2, before ECM and any descent, which the survivor count, the ECM price and the usable mass of `evaluate_descent` already assume, `ADAPTIVE.md` §5); all its prime factors are `<= 2^{185}`; it has `<= 11` prime factors in `(2^{59}, 2^{n_A}]`, `n_A = 164` (A children), and `<= 1` in `(2^{n_A}, 2^{185}]` (B child). **(R)** A child of size `s` in the row `[b_lo, b_hi)` of `plan59` (side `g`; its 31 rows tile `[59, 215)`, `rows_tile_ok`) costs that row's values, unchanged: expected work `W` (with its subtree), Boole failure bound `PHI`, slots `CC`. **(A)** Works add and failure bounds add (union bound, no independence).

**Pricing.** Before, each of the `12` children was charged the worst row below its size cap (kept as `w_dag_one_wc`, `phi_dag_one_wc`, `c_det_one_wc`, `e_runs_per_log_wc`; caps `11 x 164 + 185 = 1989` bits against `tau = 1225`). Now `w_dag_one`, `phi_dag_one`, `c_det_one` are `min(M_V, worst row)`, rounded outward, for `V = W, PHI, CC` separately. `M_V` is the maximum of `sum V[r_i]` over all multisets of `<= 11` rows with `b_lo < n_A` (A) and `<= 1` row overlapping `(n_A, 185)` (B) for which the lower row ends `floor(b_lo)` (B: `floor(max(b_lo, n_A))`) of the rows with `b_lo >= x_EA` sum to `<= tau`; rows with `b_lo < x_EA` are free (their children are stripped with the small primes first), and rounding down only enlarges the set. `boot_gate_knapsack` computes `M_V` by an exact integer DP and returns an argmax; no monotonicity of `V` in the size is used (the row values are not monotone). Values and argmax rows: `ADAPTIVE.md` §5.

**Validity (deterministic).** (i) Every descending root has a configuration in the set `C` of (G). (ii) Each child size lies in one row, so the child costs `<= W[r]`, fails with probability `<= PHI[r]` and takes `CC[r]` slots (R). (iii) The feasible set of the DP contains the row assignment of every `c` in `C` (counts `<= 11` / `<= 1`; A rows `b_lo < n_A`; B rows overlap `(n_A, 185)`; every child above `x_EA` is charged `floor(b_lo) <=` its size, every child below `x_EA` nothing, and the sizes above `x_EA` sum to `log2 cof <= tau`), so `M_V >= V(c)` for every admissible root. (iv) With `wM = w_dag_one`, `phiM = phi_dag_one`: given its configuration a run fails with probability `<= sum PHI <= phiM` (Boole) and its descent costs `<= wM`; the runs are i.i.d. and `N`, the number of runs until success, is a stopping time, so Wald gives `E[N] <= 1/(1 - phiM)` and the expected work `<= tree + E[N] (trial/prob + over + wM)`, the evaluated formula. The bounds use the separate maxima `wM`, `phiM`; a joint maximum of the work and the failure bound of one configuration is not used. Used: (G), (R), (A); the previous pricing used (R), (A) and the child counts of (G), and the budget `tau` of (G) is the early abort that the model already used. **Not used:** monotonicity of the row values, independence of the children, any distribution of the children's sizes (a Mertens-type premise would be a new probabilistic premise). **Scope:** KNAP prices the children of the boot root, its prime factors above `2^{59}` (a child below `x_EA` uses none of the budget `tau`, the rough part `cof` of (G) being the product of the prime factors above `x_EA`; every child is priced by its row). The stripping of the rational primes below `2^{59}` and the recovery of the prime ideals and valuations by Steps A-E of `ADAPTIVE.md` §1.2 are the base's, unchanged, with the base's premises.

**Check, limit.** `boot_gate_knapsack_ok` (default part; block `boot_gate_knapsack`) is the conjunction of 10 flags: parameters re-derived from the stored data; rows tile; worst rows re-derived; the DP reproduces the exported bounds; the argmax is feasible with unrounded sizes and attains them; each bound `<=` its worst row (exact rationals); the DP equals exhaustive enumeration on 7 fixed reduced instances (real rows, non-monotone values, edges at `x_EA` and `n_A`, tight budgets) and on 48 pseudo-random ones. `KNAP_ON` `= False` restores the `*_wc` values (ablation state `KNAP_off`: claim `82.241131433`). **Limit:** the gate is executed only in the miniature, as every boot step; nothing of the boot is executed at production size (cost model); the DP and its check run in the default run on the real rows.
