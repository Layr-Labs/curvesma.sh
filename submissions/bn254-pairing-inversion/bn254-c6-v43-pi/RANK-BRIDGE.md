# `RANK-BRIDGE.md` — Exact 1D-Kernel Rank Bridge (`H-R`, `H-X`, Shifted $\sigma$-Basis Extraction, & Conditional Schwartz-Zippel)

## 1. The Three Separated Premises

Let $M_{\mathrm{full}} \in \mathbb{Z}[\omega]^{R \times U'}$ be the raw relation matrix over order-6 $\tau$-orbits ($\tau = \operatorname{Frob}^{10}$, $\chi = p^{10} \bmod r$, $\chi^2 - \chi + 1 \equiv 0 \pmod r$), augmented by $s_{\mathrm{sm}} = 5$ Schirokauer columns $S_{\mathrm{full}} = [W_f(P_\chi \Lambda_f) \mid W_g(P_\chi \Lambda_g)] \in \mathbb{F}_r^{R \times 5}$ ($\alpha^{\epsilon_f} \bmod r^2$ with $\epsilon_f = r^2 - 1$ on $K_f$ where $f_{M1}$ splits as $[2,2,2,2]$ across all 6 CRT components of $h \bmod r$, and $\alpha^{\epsilon_g} \bmod r^2$ with $\epsilon_g = r - 1$ on $K_g$ where $g_{M1} = ((u+1)w+2)a^2 + b^2$ splits as $[1,1]$ across all 6 CRT components with $\gcd(N((u+1)w+2), r) = 1$; $\chi$-eigenspace dims $4, 1$, uniform random $W_f \in \mathbb{F}_r^{4 \times 48}, W_g \in \mathbb{F}_r^{1 \times 12}$; compression loses rank w.p. $\le 5/r < 2^{-250}$ under `H-SM`, in `FIX-U`). After singleton/clique purge, degree-preserving trim with immediate per-drop BFS `repurge_stack` back-sub ($< 2^{55}$ ops in `misc`, **0 post-peel cols lost** live), zero-column-loss `_dc`-guarded Markowitz merge ($W_{\mathrm{avg}} \le 100.5$, **0 post-merge cols lost**, `merge_backsub_errors = 0` live), and Boudot order-labeled unit-lower-triangular pivots $L_{\mathrm{rec}}$, the live system on $D = N_l + 5$ unscreened rows has exact Schur-complement operator $M_{\mathrm{prod}} = M_{\mathrm{live}} - E_{\mathrm{live}} L_{\mathrm{rec}}^{-1} B_{\mathrm{rec}}$, $S_{\mathrm{prod}} = S_{\mathrm{live}} - E_{\mathrm{live}} L_{\mathrm{rec}}^{-1} S_{\mathrm{rec}}$, $A = [M_{\mathrm{prod}} \mid S_{\mathrm{prod}}] \in \mathbb{F}_r^{D \times D}$, and zero-padded $B = [M_{\mathrm{prod}} \mid 0]$.

1. **`H-R` (1D Kernel):** $\operatorname{rank}(M_{\mathrm{prod}}) = N_l$ and $\operatorname{rank}(A) = D - 1$, so $V = \operatorname{Im}(A)$ has $\dim V = D - 1$ and $\ker(A) = \operatorname{span}_{\mathbb{F}_r}\{(w_{\mathrm{true}}, c_{\mathrm{true}})\}$ with $c_{\mathrm{true}} \ne 0$ ($L_{\mathrm{rec}} w_{\mathrm{rec}} = -(B_{\mathrm{rec}} w_{\mathrm{true}} + S_{\mathrm{rec}} c_{\mathrm{true}})$).
2. **`H-X` (Padded BW Controllability, Observability, & Shifted $\sigma$-Basis Extraction):** The algorithm draws dense uniform 64-bit $Z \in \mathbb{F}_r^{N_l \times (n-5)}$, $Y = [S_1,\dots,S_5, B Z] \in \mathbb{F}_r^{D \times n}$, and block-diagonal $X \in \mathbb{F}_r^{m \times D}$ placing the $5$ pad coords in distinct blocks $0..4$ with uniform 64-bit entries. At $d = \lceil D/n \rceil + 1 + \lceil D/(128n) \rceil$, $e = \lceil D/m \rceil + 1 + \lceil D/(128m) \rceil$: `H-X` asserts that the following ranks are attained by some $(X, Z)$ with these supports (item 3). **Controllability** $\operatorname{rank}(C_d) = N_l$ for $C_d = [B Z, B Y, \dots, B^d Y]$; **Observability** $O_e = [X; X B; \dots; X B^{e-1}]$ is injective on $V$ ($\operatorname{rank}(O_e A) = D - 1$).
   - **Recovery Theorem:** Let $R_d = [Y, B Y, \dots, B^d Y]$, $H = [X B^{i+j} Y]_{0 \le i < e,\; 0 \le j \le d} = O_e R_d$. *(Existence)* $(w, c) \in \ker(A)\setminus\{0\}$ has $c \ne 0$ and $S_{\mathrm{prod}} c = -M_{\mathrm{prod}} w \in \operatorname{Im}(B) = \operatorname{span}(C_d)$, giving $R_d f = 0 \implies H f = 0$ with $f_0[:5] = c \ne 0$. *(Soundness)* Any $f$ with $H f = 0$ and $c = f_0[:5] \ne 0$ has $R_d f \in V \cap \ker(O_e) = \{0\}$, so $\bar{w} = Z e_0 + \sum_{i=1}^d (B^{i-1} Y f_i)[:N_l]$ satisfies $(\bar{w}, c) \in \ker(A)$.
   - **Shifted Beckermann–Labahn $\sigma$-Basis Extraction (`gen_at_cap` + `la`):** Let $L = d + e < L_{\mathrm{cap}}$, $A(z) = \sum_{k=0}^{L-1} A_k z^k \in \mathbb{F}_r[z]^{m \times n}$, $F(z) = [A(z) \mid -I_m]$, shift $\vec{s} = (0^n, 1^m)$. With $g(z) = \sum_{j=0}^d f_{d-j} z^j$ and $q(z) = (A(z)g(z)) \bmod z^d$, $H f = 0 \iff F(z)(g,q)^T \equiv 0 \pmod{z^L}$ with $\vec{s}\text{-deg} \le d$. By Beckermann–Labahn (1994 Thm. 4.1), an order-$L$ $\vec{s}$-minimal $\sigma$-basis $V(z)$ ($k = n+m$, $\vec{s}$-degrees $\delta_\ell$) expands $w^* = (g^*, q^*)^T = \sum_{\delta_\ell \le d} c_\ell(z) v^{(\ell)}(z)$ ($\deg c_\ell \le d-\delta_\ell$); taking $[z^d]$ on rows $0..4$ gives $c_{\mathrm{true}} = \sum_{\delta_\ell \le d} ([z^{d-\delta_\ell}]c_\ell)([z^{\delta_\ell}]g^{(\ell)}[:5]) \ne 0$, proving **deterministically** that some retained column $\delta_\ell \le d$ has $f^{(\ell)}_0[:5] \ne 0$ (NO dense fallback).
   - **Strict `PM-Basis` cost (`gen_at_cap`):** $\lceil\log_2 L\rceil \le \lceil\log_2(L_{\mathrm{cap}})\rceil = 38$ levels ($L < L_{\mathrm{cap}} \approx 2^{37.35}$ at `bw=(768,32)`); since $s(r-1)^2 < 2^{546.3} < (2^{64})^9$, 9-limb Kronecker packing gives one length-$18s$ integer convolution (`fft_F(18s)`) + 9-limb Barrett mod-$r$ ($\le 640s$ Ir), so total work $\le 4k^3\lceil\log_2(L_{\mathrm{cap}})\rceil(\mathrm{fft}_F(18L_{\mathrm{cap}})+640L_{\mathrm{cap}}) = \texttt{gen\_at\_cap} \le 2^{74.912464}$ units.
3. **Conditional Schwartz-Zippel:** `H-X` is a statement about the fixed production operator, not about the draw: treat the entries of $Z$ and the entries of $X$ on its block-diagonal support as indeterminates; `H-X` says that for the fixed $B$, $A$ and $S_1..S_5$ some $N_l$-minor of $C_d$ (a polynomial in $Z$) and some $(D-1)$-minor of $O_e A$ (a polynomial in $X$) are not identically zero, i.e. some choice of $(X, Z)$ attains both ranks. Their product is then a nonzero polynomial of total degree $< 4 N_{\mathrm{sw,cap}}$ in the draws, and Schwartz-Zippel over the uniform 64-bit draws bounds the probability that the drawn $(X, Z)$ makes it vanish by $4 N_{\mathrm{sw,cap}} / 2^{64} = 1.17266 \times 10^{-6}$ (`main_matrix` in `FIX-U`). The toy system of §2 shows such minors are nonzero for its own operator; for the production operator this is the declared premise `H-X`.
