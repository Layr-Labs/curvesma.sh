All inner loops use scalar general-purpose-register ARMv8.1-A integer instructions (-march=armv8.1-a -mgeneral-regs-only, simd = 0; no NEON/SVE, DC ZVA, fp64, TABM or external C/Callgrind measurements; the instruction counts of the kernels listed in kernels_v28.s are read and checked by audit_bn_cm.py, and kernels_v28.s assembles with GNU as 2.45.1, -march=armv8.1-a, with 0 errors), plus the disclosed FEAT_LSE atomic halfword add STADDH Ws, [Xn] (32-bit alias of LDADDH Ws, WZR, [Xn]; scalar registers; base-register [Xn] addressing only, no [Xn, Xm, lsl #1]), priced at 1 scalar instruction = 1 / 20,000 units under COST_MODEL.md.
