   - Rule. Each root attempt draws fresh uniform e in [0, r-1] (`T = phi_iso(z*g^e)`, uniform on the order-r subgroup), forms the 12-D boot lattice {(a, b) : b = R a mod p} from the six rows (0, p e_i) followed by the six rows (e_k, R Z_K-coordinates mod p, centred), reduces it with the 2-stage 11/40 -> 9/10 Bareiss LLL with weights (S_A, 1) (the basis, order and weights of the input-uniform bound, <= 2^{40} Ir) and lists the 144 fixed-rule vectors {b_k} U {b_i +- b_j} in index order. A vector is KEPT when it passes the pre-norm gate (every a-coordinate of bit length < 128 and every b-coordinate of bit length < 160, i.e. |a_k| < 2^{127}, |b_k| < 2^{159}), is O_K-primitive, and its exact |N_g| = |N_K(u w a^2 - b^2)| differs from the |N_g| of every earlier vector that passed the gate (primitive or not). No vector is selected or ordered by its norm. A wave stores e once per lattice (32 B) and, with each kept vector, its lattice's position in the wave (5 B), so the successful vector's e is read back for x = (vlog(T) - e vlog(g)) vlog(g)^{-1}. Why the equality rule: the boot lattice is an O_K-module, so unit multiples (eps a, eps b) of a vector lie in it with the same |N_g|; on the stored production sample 85.297 of the 144 vectors are kept on average (minimum 62).
