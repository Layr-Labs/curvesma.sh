## Coefficients

The seed is 32 bytes. It is not expanded from a 64-bit word. Coefficient i is SHAKE256(seed || uint64be(i) || the two bytes v3), squeezed to 64 bytes. The eight index values 0 through 7 produce eight digests, each wider than 400 bits, so the image of one coefficient is not a set of size 2^64. Under the random-oracle behavior of SHAKE256, distinct indices are independent. The integer is reduced modulo the group order by 512 conditional shift-subtracts on a 4-limb register: 9216 instructions, the counter delta of that routine. For an order q < 2^256 the statistical distance of one reduction from uniform is below q / 2^512 < 2^(-256). The program checks the squeeze against the standard SHAKE256 and checks the reduction against integer division. There are 32 jumps and two coefficients each, so the derivation costs 988484 instructions, including four loads of the seed. It is charged once, not twice, and it is not multiplied by the walk length.

## One step

The curve is BLS12-381 G1, a = 0, doubling dbl-2009-l. A step adds a precomputed affine point. The charged group cost is the larger of an affine addition and an affine doubling, both with one Fermat inversion, because an x-coordinate collision uses the doubling formula. Addition is 479125 instructions and doubling is 480076, so the step pays 480076. The inversion inside that number is the measured binary exponentiation by p - 2: 476271 instructions, 608 field multiplications plus the control and the initial conversion. A field multiplication is the 6-limb CIOS routine at 777 instructions. P-256 uses that same 4-limb routine; its price is not copied from a different prime.

The partition mixes every 64-bit limb of the canonical affine encoding and keeps five bits: 4 instructions per limb plus 7, which is 55 on this encoding. The assumption, stated as an assumption, is that this function behaves as an independent uniform hash onto 32 buckets. Teske's Corollary 5.1 needs that assumption and is not a theorem about this mix. Each step also adds the two jump coefficients in the scalar field, 58 instructions each. One step is therefore 480247 instructions. Equality of the tortoise and the hare is 48 instructions and happens once per Floyd iteration.

## Budget

Let s = ceil(sqrt(r)) = 228988810152649578064853576960394133504. The program runs 4 * s Floyd iterations, and each iteration performs three additions (tortoise once, hare twice). That is 915955240610598312259414307841576534016 iterations and 2747865721831794936778242923524729602048 additions. Walk instructions: 1319698235164103331721929281180757212868378624.

Edlyn Teske, On random walks for Pollard's rho method, Mathematics of Computation 70 (2001), 809-825, DOI 10.1090/S0025-5718-00-01213-8, defines L as the number of iterations until her match finder succeeds, divided by sqrt(|G|). Equation (2.2) gives the random-mapping comparison 1.13 * sqrt(pi / 2) = 1.416, and L0 is that constant. Corollary 5.1 says that if the partition is an independent hash onto r >= 16 buckets, the average of L is at most 1.45 for every prime order. This walk uses r = 32 and Floyd's finder, which is not her finder. The corollary is used only as an average-case statement under the hash assumption above.

Floyd's tortoise meets the hare in fewer than mu + lambda iterations. The random-mapping density of x = (mu + lambda) / sqrt(|G|) is x exp(-x^2 / 2), so P(x > 4) = exp(-16/2) = exp(-8). Bernstein and Lange, in Computing small discrete logarithms faster, treat a base-(g, h) r-adding walk as the shape that simulates a random walk when r is large, and they point back to Teske for that requirement. This program does not use the negation map, so it does not rely on a negation-map theorem.

## Other lines, and what is not charged as a reserve

Jump setup is 32 Jacobian scalar multiplications of P and of Q, using dbl and madd, then one batch inversion of the 32 sums: 267770269 instructions. The output is one scalar inversion and one multiplication. Field setup is the one-time Newton iteration for n0'. The total R is 1319698235164103331721929281180757213137296332. Nothing in the sum is a constant that is supposed to stand in for per-record work.

Peak memory is 32 affine jump points plus 8192 bytes of scratch: 11264 bytes, log2 ceiling 13.459432. Challenge-independent preprocessing is the field setup and the generator loads, which divide down to 1 and a log2 ceiling of 0.000000. The jumps depend on Q, so they stay in W and are not called preprocessing. Advice is 0 bytes.

## What the declared program checks

It remeasures the counters, rebuilds W, and requires the score numerator. It recovers eight secrets on a prime-order curve of order 9967 with this partition and this sign, and it draws a fresh 32-byte seed and a fresh secret for every trial of the plan above. The negated formula is checked on every trial.
