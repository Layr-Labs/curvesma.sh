## 1. Flaw 1 - the key census runs to the width where the trend is visible, and kappa_w is derived

The verdict: *"Uniformity of v*y_j on H does not imply uniform truncated coordinate keys ... Since
v2(m)=12, this cannot equal exactly 2^-167 ... Its six-challenge, coarse-width maxima do not
establish kappa=1.932 for the production 168-bit baby keys."*

**(a) The exact object, defined.** For `N(k) = |{h in H : key_w([h]P0) = k}|` and `D_w = 2^(w-1)`
(the even representative's low bit is always zero), the challenge-space expectation is an identity,
not a model:

    E_v[#collisions] = P_all * sum_k (N(k)/m)^2 = P_all * kappa_w / D_w,
    kappa_w := D_w * sum_k (N(k)/m)^2 = 1 + D_w * sum_k (N(k) - m/D_w)^2 / m^2 >= 1.

`v = s^d` is uniform on `H` (kernel size `gcd(d, r-1) = d = 2^20`), so for each fixed giant element
`y_j` the product `v*y_j` is uniform on `H` and `Pr[key_i = key(v*y_j)] = N(key_i)/m`; summing over
the `N^2` pairs gives the line above with no independence assumed.

**(b) The v2(m)=12 part, derived exactly.** `m = 2^12 * u` with `u` odd, so for every `w >= 14`

    m mod 2^(w-1) = 2^12 * (u mod 2^(w-13)) != 0,

and a partition of `m` into `D_w = 2^(w-1)` equal parts is **impossible**: `kappa_w > 1` strictly.
The smallest excess over all partitions (all `N(k)` in `{floor(m/D), ceil(m/D)}`) is
`s(D-s)/m^2` with `s = m mod D`. At the production width `s = m mod 2^167`, and that floor is
`s(D-s)/m^2 = 2^-137.77` (`certificate.json:key_census.model.forced_remainder_v2_12`). This is the
precise, quantitative form of the reviewer's objection: exact `2^-167` uniformity is impossible, and
the amount by which it fails is at most `2^-137.77` even for the most equal partition conceivable.

**(c) The derived width law, and the birthday term.** The order-3 automorphism fixes `y` and the key
identifies `y` with `p-y`, so the `mu6` orbit contributes 6 `H`-elements per key and the effective
number of independent y-draws is `m/6`. Uniform-hash dispersion (Poisson multiplicity) then gives

    kappa_w(model) = 1 + D_w/(m/6) = 1 + 6 * 2^(w-1) / m,

which is `1 + 2^-65.272` at `w = 168` (`certificate.json:key_census.model.production`). Note what
this formula does *not* say: the forced `2^12` remainder does not multiply every key's multiplicity
by `2^12` - a uniform multiplicity factor cancels in the ratio `kappa_w`, and only the *dispersion*
of the multiplicity enters. That is why the width trend is flat at `1` rather than `1 + 2^12/2^w`;
the census below confirms the flat form and rejects the multiplicative one.

**(d) The measurement runs to 30 bits.** `census-key-v20.json` counts exact cross-side collisions at
`w = 10..30` on the challenge-faithful object at `2^16` records/side (`P_all = 2^32`, 3 challenges),
plus the v19 size (`2^12` records/side, 6 challenges) for continuity:

| w | 10 | 14 | 18 | 22 | 25 | 26 | 27 | 28 | 29 | 30 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| predicted | 2^23 | 2^19 | 2^15 | 2^11 | 2^8 | 2^7 | 64 | 32 | 16 | 8 |
| observed/predicted | 1.00005 | 1.00023 | 1.00274 | 1.00749 | 1.00391 | 0.96354 | 0.92188 | 0.95833 | 0.95833 | 1.04167 |
| model | 1.000000 | 1.000000 | 1.000000 | 1.000000 | 1.000000 | 1.000000 | 1.000000 | 1.000000 | 1.000000 | 1.000000 |

The model value is `1.000000` at every tested width because `6*2^(w-1)/m <= 2^-190` there; the
measured ratio tracks `1` to within `1/sqrt(predicted)` at every width, which is what the thinning
counts in the last four columns show. Per-width fit residuals ship beside the forced-remainder term
(`certificate.json:key_census.model.per_width`), and the within-side multiplicity histogram - the
verdict's `|{h in H : key([h]P0) = k}|` - is shipped per width as
`rows[*].within_side_a` (distinct keys, max multiplicity, mean multiplicity).

**(e) The charge, and why it is not the model value.** The ledger charges

    kappa = 1.231 = 1 + max_w ucb_excess(w) over cells with predicted >= 256

(`certificate.json:key_census.kappa_float`), the one-sided 3-sigma envelope at the widest
well-populated width (`w = 25`, `sigma = 1/sqrt(256) = 0.0625`). That is `2^64` times larger than the
model's production value `1 + 2^-65.272`: the charge is deliberately the *noise* envelope, not the
formula. `E[#bad] = 1.231 * N^2 / 2^167 = 2^64.6005`, and the Markov bound on the 2^104-instruction
candidate cap is `2^-19.399`. v19 charged `1.932` for `2^-18.749`; the new charge is strictly smaller
and the cell band it dominates is strictly wider.

**(f) The reviewer's distribution objection, measured.** "Census samples uniform H factors rather
than the attack's band-limited H-prime factors." At production index `d = 2^20` H-primes are
infeasible (`2^-25.3` density), so the control runs at `d = 2^4` **on the production curve**, where
band-limited H-primes are affordable: 4096 records/side, both distributions, widths 10-24
(`certificate.json:key_census.bandlimited_control`). Band-limited ratios are
`1.002, 1.005, 1.006, 1.010, 1.141, 1.313, 1.125`; uniform-`H'` ratios at the same widths are
`0.997, 0.988, 0.978, 1.006, 1.023, 1.094, 1.375`. The two curves coincide within noise, so
band-limitation is not a source of key bias in the object the attack builds.

## 2. Flaw 2 - the ratio ranges are reported over ALL cells, with no filter

v19's advertised ranges held only after selecting `predicted >= 16`. There is no such selection here.
Over **every** cell with a defined ratio (`census-key-v20.json`):

| row | cells | min | max |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| faithful (H-members, challenge-scaled) | 37 | 0.833333 | 1.333333 |
| control (v18 object, unscaled) | 16 | 0.0 | 0.992889 |

(`certificate.json:key_census.ranges_all_cells`.) The extremes are sampling, and the package says so
where it can be checked: the faithful minimum sits at a cell with 4 predicted collisions and the
maximum at a cell with 1, where the Poisson sd is 50-100%. The `predicted >= 16` view is retained as
a **disclosed secondary row** (`faithful 0.921875..1.041667`) and is never used for a charge. The
`kappa` charge uses `predicted >= 256` cells and names that threshold in
`certificate.json:key_census.kappa_definition`.

## 3. Flaw 3 - coverage at two geometric scales, and the exp(-K) sentence is gone

**(a) The sentence the verdict quoted is deleted.** No part of this package asserts or implies that
linearity of expectation yields an `exp(-K)` occupancy law. `exp(-K)` appears only as the *design
target* at the design point, with `K_eff` diagonal-corrected; what is charged is the measured
residual (`certificate.json:coverage_grid.expectation.method`, and the builder asserts the v19 phrase
is absent).

**(b) The grid is wider and runs at two scales of the same SHAPE.** Eight bases per side (v19: four),
at `d in {8, 64}`, two fields, with list sizes and field size both doubled between scales so `K_cand`
is matched and the pool:walk geometry is preserved:

| scale | d | K_cand | D | pred_eff | rowA | rowB |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2^23, 8/side | 8 | 0.5012 | 407640 | 405039 | -0.007622 | **-0.002480** |
| 2^23, 8/side | 64 | 1.6869 | 106188 | 105158 | -0.062318 | -0.007856 |
| 2^25, 16/side | 8 | 0.5013 | 1622178 | 1620035 | -0.005656 | **-0.000511** |
| 2^25, 16/side | 64 | 1.6875 | 419448 | 420549 | -0.052386 | +0.002100 |

**(c) The charge.** `|rowB|` is charged **once**, at the production design point, as an exact
Fraction: `0.00247964` (`certificate.json:coverage_grid.charge`). Two disclosures ride with it.
First, v19 charged `maxA + maxB` additively; the diagonal term is already inside `K_eff` and hence
inside `pred_eff`, so that sum counts it twice - `maxA` is shipped as a diagnostic
(`0.00762195`), not as a second charge. Second, the `d = 64` cells sit at `K = 1.687`, not at the
production `K = 0.51`, because the fixture's list-length floor (`nw >= 24`) cannot reach `K = 0.51`
at `m = 131118`; both cells are printed with their `K` and their `|rowB|` and are **not** charged.

**(d) The scale-stability table is what is claimed, and nothing more.** `|rowB|` falls at both
indices across the scales (ratios `0.206` and `0.267`), i.e. the residual law is stable in the
direction claimed, at twice the list size. No cross-scale extrapolation, no `m`-law and no
`sqrt(m)` normalisation is used: the charge is the toy maximum and the direction claim is the
stability table (`certificate.json:coverage_grid.stability`).

## 4. Flaw 4 - the gap is a coin probability, and routing loads are measured

**(a) The 2^-40 "modelling allowance" is gone.** The construction is randomized and self-checking:
the walk-band start is derived from a seed (an algorithm coin), the construction verifies
`max gap <= 2^7.7 * mu` on the drawn list, and re-draws on violation. The deployed list therefore
satisfies the gap condition **by construction**; the only randomness left is the number of draws.
The census measures the coin: **128 independent seeds** (two scales x 64), each scanning a
seed-derived disjoint band for consecutive H-primes, recording the gap distribution and the KS fit
(`census-rest-v20.json:gap`, `certificate.json:gap`):

| scale | seeds | mean gap bits | max/mean max | KS pass | seeds over 2^7.7 |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2^36 | 64 | 7.43-7.80 | 10.732 | 0.984 | 0 |
| 2^38 | 64 | 7.51-7.79 | 10.464 | 1.000 | 0 |

The production requirement `max/mean <= 2^7.7 = 208` is met by every seed, with margin `2^4.276`.
With the rule-of-three bound on 0 failures, `q <= 3/128`, and 10 allowed draws, the success debit is
`q^10 = 2^-54.150`; the expected draw count is `1.024`, costing `2^97.782` instructions - a new
ledger row (`gap_retry`) that is `2^-19` of the total and does not move the claim at four decimals.

**(b) Routing loads are charged at the measured maximum.** Route widths are scaled down so the
fixture has measurable occupancy (`mean >= 4` records/class), and the max/mean ratio is measured
against the uniform-occupancy formula:

| route bits | classes | mean load | max load | max/mean | prefix pairs | pred | ucb ratio |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 9 | 512 | 8.0 | 18 | 2.25 | 33170 | 32768 | 1.029 |
| 12 | 4096 | 1.0 | 6 | 6.00 | 4110 | 4096 | 1.050 |
| 15 | 32768 | 0.125 | 3 | 24.00 | 506 | 512 | 1.120 |

Loads are charged at `2.25`, the measured ratio at the largest mean; the two rows at `mean <= 1` are
disclosed and not charged, since a max load cannot be below 1. Prefix pairs are charged at `1.120`,
giving `2^84.47` against the `2^95` run cap - a `10.53`-bit margin with the measured factor already
applied (`certificate.json:debit.prefix_charged_log2`). The cap-abort event is the same seed-coin
re-draw as the gap, with probability `2^-54.150`.

## 6. Scope

Analytic bound, no full-size run; instruction counts itemised and toy-counted rather than measured on
hardware. Named premises: `H3occ-scale` (the declared residual bound `0.00247964`), `H3key` (the
width law of section 1(c), verified where measurable and charged at the widest tested envelope),
`H3gap` (the seed-coin construction of section 4(a)). The certificate recomputes the ledger and the
declared formulas from raw evidence; it does **not** independently establish production resource
bounds, run the production attack, or verify the premises.
