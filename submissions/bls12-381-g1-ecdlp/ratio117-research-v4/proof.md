## 1. Measured side-output kernel: 929 instructions per lane, worst path, with explicit fixed-call row

side_lanes(args, n) (measure/side_lanes.S) keeps the k16 interface: args[i] = {out, P, T, inv}; it writes fully reduced x' of P-T to out+0, y' to out+48, the parity key (limbs 1..3 of whichever of y' and p-y' is even, a38cb931) to out+96; args[n] = {NULL, NULL} ends the lane loop. Inputs: x1, y1, x2, y2 canonical Montgomery residues (P a kernel output, restart root or strand start; T a gap-table entry), y2 != 0 (G1 has odd prime order), inv = 1/(x2-x1) mod p from the batch round, below 1.17p but not necessarily canonical (s27). Each gap-table entry stores v2 = p - x2 at T+96, n2 = p - y2 at T+144 (192-byte entries; +21/entry in the gap-table step, TABLE_BK 48 <= 50; re-walk rebuild 2000+50; memory GMAX x 192; hoisted stride holds 192). Base-point/strand-start entries get v/n inside their scalar-mult charge (SM = 8192000/point, ~4x a double-and-add).

Arithmetic (unchanged; bounds at the s27 inverse range). With a + p < 3p < 2^383, every pre-shift sum is below 2^447, every shifted accumulator below 2^383; every in-round partial sum is a sub-sum of nonnegative pre-shift terms.
