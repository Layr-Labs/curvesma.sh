## 8. One-sided early abort: mechanism, expected-work bound and accounting

The baby side emits fully, exactly as in the base schedule. The giant side emits from a random cyclic offset and the run aborts at the first verified-true candidate. Only giant-phase emission work is scored in expectation; every other row is at its maximum (comparisons, candidate rows, sieves, planner, distribution, owner initialisation are unchanged from the base), and every failure path is charged at its maximum with no conditioning on success.

### 8.1 Expected work is the scored metric

rules.md requires charging "all preprocessing, advice construction, trials and failures, randomness, arithmetic, sorting/lookup, transfers, verification, output and amplification" over the stated probability space of uniform challenges and fresh algorithm coins. The "trials and failures" instruction has force only under expectation: it forbids reporting work conditioned on success (the lucky path) and requires the unconditional mean including failure paths. A worst-case-only reading would make that instruction vacuous (a maximum already covers every path) and would contradict the design's mean-based caps (comparison caps at 1.01x the mean, link caps at 2x the mean, pool at 1.29x the mean), whose overflow events are failure-budgeted rather than worst-case-bounded. The base line's full-emission ledger is a valid (loose) upper bound on expected work; this package scores a tighter valid upper bound. The expectation is over (uniform t in H, fresh coins S and below); the emitted sets B, G are fixed challenge-independent sets as in the base.

### 8.2 Mechanism

Random offset. Before the giant phase, one uniform S in {0, ..., N-1} is sampled and distributed with the inputs (8 bytes per node, offset-distribution row). Giant lanes consume virtual emission indices in rotated order: virtual index v is served at schedule position (v+S) mod N, mapped to (base, segment, offset) through the segment directory's cumulative offsets by binary search (charged at 200 instructions per giant segment handoff, expected handoff count plus LANES). Rotation permutes only service order; full bases walk identical sets, the greedy padding lemma is order-independent, and the partial last base walks the same count in its rotated window (a different window on one base of 2^61, H1 impact below 2^-58). Baby order is unchanged. Root sampling of S (rejection from 117 bits plus packing) is charged explicitly (128 instructions) with distribution.

Per-subwave verification and abort. Giant waves split into subwaves of SUBW=2^24 steps. After each giant subwave, its candidates are delivered, re-walked and tested exactly as in the base, batched per subwave; per-candidate cost is identical and delivery/re-walk/test rows stay at the 2^44 cap. Re-walk/verify work runs on the generating lanes under static 7:1 time-slicing with emission (makespan effect only; work unchanged), giving re-walk throughput 2^14 candidates per subwave against mean arrivals 2^9.2, so no verification queue forms (utilization 2^-4.8, expected queuing below 2^-10 subwave, absorbed below). Caps are enforced per subwave with draining; per-subwave means are 2^10 below phase means at identical thresholds, so the union over subwaves of the super-exponentially smaller tails sits strictly below the base phase tails and H3 holds a fortiori. One found-flag bit rides in the barrier arrival word (count + generation + flag fit in 64 bits): zero extra instructions. Each node checks its release flag per giant subwave (charged in the abort-signal row); on found, the giant phase stops everywhere. Abort latency past a true record is 1 (lookup) + 2 (reverse delivery) + 1 (re-walk) + 1 (tests) + 1 (found reduction and broadcast) = 6 subwaves, plus 2 drain subwaves of in-flight records; overshoot is charged at 8 subwaves at the full giant per-point price. Link words, barriers and the barrier tree are charged per subwave (giant) and per wave (baby).

### 8.3 Expected giant emission: E[F] <= 0.81

Fix the emitted sets B, G (|B|=|G|=N) and let J(t) be the number of true pairs for challenge t (pairs (x,y) with x*y^-1*t^-1 in U). E_t[J] = N^2 x 6/m = K exactly: each pair covers 6/m of H uniformly as t varies (x, y in H by the class construction), with no heuristic. P(J=0) = P(t not in R) = 1-|R|/m <= e^-K+0.0005 by H1 (0.60996 at KT=0.49514; the realized K >= KT only tightens e^-K).

Conditional on the sets and t with J >= 1, let p_1 be the (fixed) emission index of the first true pair in plan order. The arrival schedule position of virtual index (p_1-S) mod N, over uniform independent S, is uniform over plan positions marginally, so E_S[first arrival] <= E_S[any single true arrival] = sched-mean (a minimum never exceeds any element; no independence across true pairs is needed). The greedy schedule emits at full rate (LANES records per step per node) until at most SEGLEN tail steps (padding lemma: lanes finish within one segment of each other), so all but at most TAIL_REC = GENS x LANES x SEGLEN x 2 tail records arrive at rate-constant times with mean at most T/2 (T the full-emission makespan), and each tail record arrives at most T/2 later than that mean. Hence sched-mean <= T/2 x (1 + TAIL_REC/N), i.e. E_S[first-true fraction | J>=1] <= (1 + TAIL_REC/N)/2 with TAIL_REC/N = 2^-25.6. The tail term is charged explicitly in the tail-slack row (TAIL_REC records at the full giant per-point price, about twice the slack) and is additionally inside the EFRAC margin. With full emission on J=0: E[F] <= (1+P(J=0))/2 + TAIL_REC/2N. The ledger claims EFRAC=81/100 for the main term (1+e^-KT+0.0005)/2 = 0.80499278... (asserted by evidence.py to sit below 0.81), with margin 0.005. No new probabilistic premise is introduced: uniformity comes from the algorithm's own coins S (independent of t by construction), the mean bound from the padding lemma, and P(J=0) from H1.

The toy's own miss rate 0.55 predicts (1+0.55)/2 = 0.775 via the section-8.3 formula, matching the measured 0.78: the toy validates the mechanism formula E[F] <= (1+P(miss))/2, while the production P(miss) <= 0.61 comes from H1, not from the toy. Rotation neither helps nor hurts the mean (both near 0.78); its production role is making uniformity exact by construction. Finite-shape evidence only, as throughout.
