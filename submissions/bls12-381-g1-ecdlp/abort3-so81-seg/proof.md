## 8. One-sided early abort: mechanism, expected-work bound and accounting

The baby side emits fully, exactly as in the base schedule. The giant side emits its bases in rotated order with segment-aligned verification rounds, and the run aborts at the first verified-true candidate. Only giant-phase emission work is scored in expectation; every other row is at its maximum (comparisons, candidate rows, sieves, planner, distribution, owner initialisation are unchanged from the base), and every failure path is charged at its maximum with no conditioning on success.

### 8.1 Expected work is the scored metric

rules.md requires charging "all preprocessing, advice construction, trials and failures, randomness, arithmetic, sorting/lookup, transfers, verification, output and amplification" over the stated probability space of uniform challenges and fresh algorithm coins. The "trials and failures" instruction has force only under expectation: it forbids reporting work conditioned on success (the lucky path) and requires the unconditional mean including failure paths. A worst-case-only reading would make that instruction vacuous (a maximum already covers every path) and would contradict the design's mean-based caps (comparison caps at 1.01x the mean, link caps at 2x the mean, pool at 1.29x the mean), whose overflow events are failure-budgeted rather than worst-case-bounded. The base line's full-emission ledger is a valid (loose) upper bound on expected work; this package scores a tighter valid upper bound. The expectation is over uniform t in H and fresh algorithm coins S (below); the emitted baby set B is the fixed challenge-independent set of the base, and the giant set is the same construction in rotated base order.

### 8.2 Mechanism: base-order rotation, greedy within-base emission, segment-aligned rounds

Rotation level. Rotation is over BASES, not emission indices: one uniform S^B over the giant base list is sampled before the giant phase (charged 128) and distributed with the inputs (8 bytes per node). Nodes service their giant bases one after another in rotated order (rotated index per base: add plus compare plus conditional subtract, charged 8 per base, all bases). Within each base, emission is the base schedule verbatim: same class plan, segment directory and cursor, greedy handoff with the listed 16-instruction restart, one gap-table load per base, same paired walks. Handoffs stay sequential per node, so no node ever holds two bases' tables; every handoff lands on a genuine restart location with full dependency state. The padding lemma applies verbatim within each base; no global rate envelope is asserted or needed. The baby side is untouched.

Verification rounds and service schedule. Emission is grouped into segment-aligned rounds: each round every lane emits one whole segment (GENS x LANES per round machine-wide); a global round barrier separates rounds. Lanes are sequential within a node (the kernel loop) and nodes barrier-synchronize with wfi, so round sync adds zero padding; round makespan varies (segments vary) and is unscored. The step-driven transport waves run unchanged underneath (charged in full). After each round barrier, every candidate received so far is verified: reverse delivery on the base path (delivery row at the 2^44 cap; per-link candidate caps as in the base, overflow the same budgeted H3 failure with smaller means), re-walk of the named segment at its generating node, scalar tests at the owner. Re-walks interleave with emission (makespan only; work covered by the cap rows); per-round capacity is the full lane count (2^65 slots against at most 2^44 candidates per round, since per-round arrivals cannot exceed the global cap on non-failure paths and overflow is the budgeted base failure), so NO verification queue can form: every round's candidates clear within a fixed deterministic pipeline with no backlog, no tails, no queuing model. Each node retains its current plus three previous gap tables (4 x GMAX x 96 bytes in gen_mem; a base lasts at least 2^13 rounds against the 2059-round pipeline below, so re-walk tables are always present and never rebuilt for latency). The found flag rides in the barrier arrival word (zero extra instructions); each node checks its release flag per round (signal row). On found, all nodes stop immediately, including mid-round.

Deterministic overshoot. The abort pipeline from a true record's emission is at most: transport lag 2 waves = at most 2048 rounds (section 4.3: the owner reads wave w-2 in wave w; a round is at least SEG lane-steps per lane), 4 reverse-delivery rounds, 2 re-walk, 2 test, 1 reduction, 1 broadcast and 1 drain round: 2059, plus 1 boundary round, for OVERSHOOT_ROUNDS = 2060 exactly. Every stage is a deterministic maximum (capacities exceed every a priori arrival bound): overshoot is 2060 rounds charged at maxima (records at full giant price, restarts per overshoot segment, one partial-base tail at ROOT extra), not a tail bound.

### 8.3 Expected giant emission: E[F] <= 0.81

Fix t with J(t) >= 1, where J(t) counts true pairs over the rotated giant set and the full baby set. Select the lexicographically-first true pair in plan order (a t-measurable selection; no independence across pairs is needed since the run stops at the minimum and a minimum never exceeds any element). Given t, that pair's giant record sits at a fixed within-base position p <= EMAX of a fixed base j; over uniform S^B each other base precedes j with probability exactly 1/2 (random rotation puts any two distinct bases in either relative order equiprobably), so expected preceding points equal (N - size_j)/2 <= N/2 exactly by pairwise symmetry (base sizes vary; symmetry needs none of it). Hence E_S[arrival points | t] <= N/2 + EMAX, i.e. fraction 1/2 + EMAX/N with EMAX/N < 2^-58 (asserted exactly). The rotated-last base walks the count-prefix window rather than the natural one; the coverage set moves by at most 2 x EMAX x N ratios (asserted 2 x EMAX x N x 200 < m), so P(J=0) <= e^-K + 0.0005 + 2^-61 by H1 applied to the rotated construction (0.60996 at KT=0.49514; realized K >= KT only tightens e^-K). With full emission on J=0: E[F] <= (1+e^-KT+0.0005)/2 + 2^-58 + 2^-62 = 0.80499278... plus absorbed terms. The ledger claims EFRAC=81/100 with explicit margin 0.005 over the main term (asserted by evidence.py), absorbing both terms with room to spare. No new probabilistic premise is introduced: uniformity over bases comes from the algorithm's own coins S^B (independent of t by construction), the mean bound from exact rotation symmetry, and P(J=0) from H1.

### 8.4 Row-by-row ledger consequences

Giant emission before the abort signal partitions into: complete bases (each a complete walked class plan, priced at the H4 floor mix by section 6), the abort-base (in progress at the signal, at most EMAX points, priced at full ROOT price), and the abort-round partials (at most one round's points, priced at full ROOT price). Complete-base points inside arrival emission are at most EFRAC x N in expectation; complete bases finishing during the pipeline are covered by the overshoot row at full price instead. Partials and the last-base prefix drag are explicit maxima that may conservatively overlap the expectation count. Pipeline emission after the signal is the deterministic 2060-round overshoot row. Per-point rows (records walk part, allowance, head-index) follow the same partition. Restarts are per serviced segment (order-independent): expected whole-segment restarts plus explicit maxima for the abort-base segments, abort-round segments, overshoot segments and full initial handoffs. Charged UNSCALED (conservative, negligible): padding (greedy within-base verbatim; partial-base padding bounded by the same per-base lemma), per-base gap tables, strand starts and fixed calls. Comparisons, cap enforcement and all candidate rows stay at the full-emission caps. Baby rows, owner initialisation, distribution, planner, traversals, sieves and root recovery are unchanged. New rows: overshoot, round barriers, abort-signal checks, offset distribution/mapping. Preprocessing is the exact baby phase; memory adds three retained gap tables per generating node.
