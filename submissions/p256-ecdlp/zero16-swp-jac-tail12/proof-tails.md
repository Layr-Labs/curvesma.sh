# Section16: correction tails and the disclosed heuristic H_T

Sites. Each incidence has seven data-dependent correction tails; all other hot instructions are path-independent. Five are weak-REDC tails (B.CC over four): forward A_l=M(A_(l-1),d_l); backward i_l=M(B_l,A_(l-1)), B_(l-1)=M(B_l,d_l), M(|dy|,i_l) and M(y-sum,i_l). Two are the fused outputs, whose13/12 cases cost5/4 more than8. The worst case4*5+5*2=30 is inside2930/4, so with no tail taken an incidence costs at most2810/4: per call (441/4-4)*n+46 forward and (2489/4-26)*n+33 fused backward, carry/flush/driver terms unchanged (unrolled and single copies share the seven tails, so the section3 remainder argument holds). The per-run worst case keeps all tails.

E-row. Let F be the tail excess over all rows of all nodes and all526 groups, run or not. Pointwise, run work is at most the tail-free rows plus F, so E[work] is at most the section15 rows at2810/4 plus E[F] (no PHI). E[F] is the sum over incidences and sites of excess times the probability the tail is taken: linearity of expectation only, with no independence between sites, incidences, rows or the stopping time and no conditional rate given any history. A defective row (probability at most d_batch) is charged30. range_ledger.py: correction_tails=NODES*q1*q2*(12+12*d_batch), plus first_prefix_tail=NODES*q1*4 for the exception below.

H_T (the one heuristic this version adds): for a non-defective row, over the uniform challenge and fresh coins, each tail of each incidence is taken with probability at most0.4, except each row's final backward update B_0=M(B_1,d_1): it represents one, so its raw value is delta=R0-p or R0 and its tail is almost always taken; first_prefix_tail charges it again in full (4) on every row. Support: (1) the Montgomery extra-reduction model (Schindler 2000; Walter-Thompson 2001): with m uniform and operands behaving as uniform residues, P(T>=R0) is about E[ab]/(p*R0): about1/4 for two full operands,1/6 with an absolute x difference,1/12 for |dy| of normalized y. (2) probe.py tails: an exact integer model of the seven tails is checked call by call against the generated listing on actual P-256 rows (executed count minus model excess is single-valued per call shape and inside the tail-free envelope), then run on2^14 incidences of random P-256 points over two Y tables: site rates0.155,0.249,0.159,0.082,0.278, fused non-8 rates0.369,0.366, every per-table rate below0.374, mean excess below7.4 against the charged12 (seed97; seeds1,193 agree). Limitations: H_T is unproved (weak operands are deterministic functions of the points); the census is finite. H_T enters only the expected-work bound; success (section6) and the per-run worst case use no tail assumption.
