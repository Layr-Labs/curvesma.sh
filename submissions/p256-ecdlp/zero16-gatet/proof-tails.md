Sites. Each incidence has five data-dependent correction tails; all other hot instructions are path-independent. The worst case5*4=20 is inside2614/4, so with no tail taken an incidence costs at most2534/4: per call (441/4-4)*n+46 forward and (2173/4-16)*n+34 backward, flush and driver terms unchanged (unrolled and single copies share the five tails). The per-run worst case keeps all tails.

E-row. Group g's tail work is executed only if T>g-1, an event fixed by H_g (section15). So E[F]<=sum_g PHI[g]*max E[F_g|H_g], F_g the tail excess of group g's rows: given H_g those rows use fresh a_k, and H_T bounds the conditional rates (linearity only; no independence between sites, incidences or rows). A defective row (given H_g, probability at most d_batch by the union bound over its fresh X) is charged20. range_ledger.py: correction_tails=sum_g PHI[g]*NODES*q_g*q2*(10+10*d_batch), first_prefix_tail=sum_g PHI[g]*NODES*q_g*4.

H_T (the one heuristic): for a non-defective row, given the history H_g before its group and over its fresh coin a_k, each tail of each incidence is taken with probability at most1/2, except each row's final backward update B_0=M(B_1,d_1): it represents one, so its raw value is delta=R0-p or R0 and its tail is almost always taken; first_prefix_tail charges it again in full (4) on every row that runs. Support: (1) the Montgomery extra-reduction model (Schindler 2000; Walter-Thompson 2001): with m uniform and operands behaving as uniform residues, P(T>=R0) is about E[ab]/(p*R0): about1/4 for two full operands,1/3 for the square of a full operand, below1/6 with an absolute x difference, about1/8 for normalized y times a full operand. (2) probe.py tails: an exact integer model of the five tails is checked call by call against the generated listing on actual P-256 rows (executed count minus model excess equals529n+21-14floor(n/8) for every backward call), then run on2^14 incidences of random P-256 points over two Y tables: site rates0.155,0.249,0.159,0.333,0.126 (forward, i, B, w, M1), every per-table rate (a fixed Y table, as under H_g) below0.34, mean excess below4.1 against the charged10 (seed97; seeds1,193 agree). Limitations: H_T is unproved (weak operands are deterministic functions of the points); the census is finite. H_T enters only the expected-work bound; success (section6) and the per-run worst case use no tail assumption.
