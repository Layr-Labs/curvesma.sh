# Section16: correction tails and the disclosed heuristic H_T

Sites. Each incidence has five data-dependent correction tails; all other hot instructions are path-independent. All five are weak-REDC tails (B.CC over four): forward A_l=M(A_(l-1),d_l); backward i_l=M(B_l,A_(l-1)), B_(l-1)=M(B_l,d_l), w_l=M(i_l,i_l) and M1_l=M(y(Y_l),w_l). The fused squares, their13/12 cases and the two slope products of earlier versions no longer exist. The worst case5*4=20 is inside2598/4, so with no tail taken an incidence costs at most2518/4: per call (433/4-4)*n+62 forward and (2165/4-16)*n+34 backward, flush and driver terms unchanged (unrolled and single copies share the five tails). The per-run worst case keeps all tails.

E-row. Let F be the tail excess over all rows of all nodes and all526 groups, run or not. Pointwise, run work is at most the tail-free rows plus F, so E[work] is at most the section15 rows at2518/4 plus E[F] (no PHI). E[F] is the sum over incidences and sites of excess times the probability the tail is taken: linearity of expectation only, with no independence between sites, incidences, rows or the stopping time and no conditional rate given any history. A defective row (probability at most d_batch) is charged20. range_ledger.py: correction_tails=NODES*q1*q2*(10+10*d_batch), plus first_prefix_tail=NODES*q1*4 for the exception below.

H_T (the one heuristic this version adds): for a non-defective row, over the uniform challenge and fresh coins, each tail of each incidence is taken with probability at most1/2, except each row's final backward update B_0=M(B_1,d_1): it represents one, so its raw value is delta=R0-p or R0 and its tail is almost always taken; first_prefix_tail charges it again in full (4) on every row. Support: (1) the Montgomery extra-reduction model (Schindler 2000; Walter-Thompson 2001): with m uniform and operands behaving as uniform residues, P(T>=R0) is about E[ab]/(p*R0): about1/4 for two full operands,1/3 for the square of a full operand, below1/6 with an absolute x difference, about1/8 for normalized y times a full operand. (2) probe.py tails: an exact integer model of the five tails is checked call by call against the generated listing on actual P-256 rows (executed count minus model excess equals527n+21-14floor(n/8) for every backward call), then run as a per-position census on2^14 incidences (256 rows of64 random P-256 points): site rates0.168,0.243,0.185,0.331,0.124 (forward, i, B, w, M1); every site at every position at most0.395 except B_0 (B site, position0, rate1: the exception above); mean excess below4.21 against the charged10 (seed97; seeds1 and193: at most0.411 and4.20). No other site has a structurally special position: at position0, i_0=1/d_0 is a generic inverse, so w_0=M(i_0,i_0) and M1_0=M(y_Y,w_0) are generic products. Limitations: H_T is unproved (weak operands are deterministic functions of the points); the census is finite. H_T enters only the expected-work bound; success (section6) and the per-run worst case use no tail assumption.
