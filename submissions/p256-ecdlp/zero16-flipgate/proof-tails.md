# Section16: correction tails and the disclosed heuristic H_T

Sites. Each X-pair has six data-dependent correction tails in the leaf passes, three per incidence; all other leaf instructions are path-independent (table lookups and folds are branch-free). All six are carry-only REDC tails (B.CC over four): forward A_j=M(A_(j-1),e_j); backward inv=M(A_(j-1),B), B<-M(e_j,B), i1=M(d2,inv), and the squares w=S(i1), w=S(i2). The worst case 3*4=12 per incidence is inside 597, so with no tail taken an incidence costs 585: per call 359/2 per pair forward and 1981/2 backward, per-Y, per-call, flush and driver terms unchanged. The per-Y kernels (chain step, Y-entry preparation) have eleven tail sites per Y; they are charged in full in the 1696 per Y and use no heuristic. The per-run worst case keeps all tails.

E-row. Let F be the tail excess of the leaf passes over all batches of all nodes and all526 groups, run or not. Pointwise, run work is at most the tail-free rows plus F, so E[work] is at most the section15 rows at585 per incidence plus E[F] (no PHI). E[F] is the sum over pairs and sites of excess times the probability the tail is taken: linearity of expectation only, with no independence between sites, pairs, batches or the stopping time and no conditional rate given any history. A defective batch (probability at most d_batch) is charged12 per incidence. flip_ledger.py: correction_tails=N*G*L*2^20*(6+6*d_batch), plus first_prefix_tail=N*G*L*8 for the two exceptions below.

H_T (the one heuristic, as in 008389f5): for a non-defective batch, over the uniform challenge and fresh coins, each tail of each of the six sites at each pair position is taken with probability at most1/2, except two structurally special positions per batch, both charged again in full (4 each, first_prefix_tail): the first forward multiply A_0=M(R,e_0), whose multiplicand is the Montgomery one R=2^256-p<2^224, and the final backward update B_(-1)=M(e_0,B_0), which represents one, so its raw value is 2^256-p or R0 and its tail is almost always taken. Support: (1) the Montgomery extra-reduction model (Schindler 2000; Walter-Thompson 2001): with m uniform and operands behaving as uniform residues, P(T>=R0) is about E[ab]/(p*R0): about1/4 for two full operands and1/3 for the square of a full operand; the folded table sums e, i2 and the canonical d2 are full residues below 2^256. (2) probe.py runs the exact integer tail model of all six sites (the model checked call by call against the executed listing: executed count minus four times the taken tails equals the measured formula on every call) on random P-256 points: 32 batches of 4 Y's by 16 pairs at seed97 give site rates0.2495 (forward),0.2373 (inv),0.2563 (B),0.2461 (i1),0.3442 and0.3208 (squares), mean excess3.31 per incidence against the charged6; the forced final update has rate1.0 and the first prefix multiply0.0, as stated. (3) A per-position census of the same model, run outside the hosted experiment with the shipped code (`python3 -c "import flip_check as fc; print(fc.census(SEED, 3000))"` for SEED 1 and 2, 3000 batches of 64 pair positions each) found every site at every position at most0.3547, except the forced final update (rate1.0); inv and the first prefix multiply at position0, whose multiplicand is the small Montgomery one, are at0.0; binomial standard error about0.009. Limitations: H_T is unproved (representatives are deterministic functions of the points); the census is finite. H_T enters only the expected-work bound; success (section6) and the per-run worst case use no tail assumption.

# Section17: run gate

First one coordinator reads a fresh 64-bit RNDR word u (a coin independent of the challenge and of all other coins) and broadcasts g=[u<K], K=8728149780331778739. If g=1 every node runs sections2-16 unchanged at L=347674640; otherwise all nodes halt and the coordinator outputs the scalar0. The gate is charged64 instructions on every run (read, compare, branch, broadcast, default output).

Success. g is independent of the run, so Pr(success)>=(K/2^64)*s_run, with s_run>=0.8242560416 the section6 bound at this L. K is the least integer with (K/2^64)*s_run>=0.39 (flip_ledger.py asserts K and K-1), giving0.3900000000000000000189.

Work. Pointwise, work is64 plus g times the run's work, so E[work]=64+(K/2^64)*E_run, with E_run the section15 total (rows, caps and H_T unchanged): 2^125.0182... units overall, 2^126.0978... for an opened run, whose per-run worst case is section15's. This is section15's expectation convention (over the challenge and all algorithm coins) with one more coin; nothing is amortized over targets, and a closed gate is charged and counted as a failure.
