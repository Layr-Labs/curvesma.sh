# Section16: correction tails and the disclosed heuristic H_T

Sites. Each X-pair has six data-dependent correction tails in the leaf passes, three per incidence; the fold tails are separate in section18; the canonical d2 correction is charged at its worst9 on both paths; remaining leaf instructions are path-independent. All six are carry-only REDC tails (B.CC over four): forward A_j=M(A_(j-1),e_j); backward inv=M(A_(j-1),B), B<-M(e_j,B), i1=M(d2,inv), and the squares w=S(i1), w=S(i2). The worst case 3*4=12 per incidence is inside571.25, so with no tail taken an incidence costs559.25 before the fold tails: per call 152 per pair forward and 1933/2 backward, per-Y, per-call, flush and driver terms unchanged. The per-Y kernels (chain step, Y-entry preparation) have eleven tail sites per Y; they are charged in full in the1720 per Y and use no heuristic. The per-run worst case keeps all tails.

E-row. Split group g's tail excess F_g=F_g^nd+F_g^d over its non-defective and defective batches. Group g's tail work is executed only if T>g-1, an event fixed by H_g (section15), so E[1[T>g-1]F_g^nd]<=PHI[g]*max E[F_g^nd|H_g]: given H_g the group's batches use fresh chain coins (c,s), and H_T bounds the conditional rate of each tail on a non-defective batch (linearity only; an identity X makes its node's batches defective, so they fall in F^d). The PHI=1 bound and the per-run worst case keep every tail on every batch.

# Section17: run gate

If u>=K the coordinator outputs the scalar0 and halts: no node is ever started, so a closed gate costs64 instructions (read, compare, branch, output). # Section12 receiver: hash join and in-record next words

The receiver (route.s receive) joins every accepted record into its home's hash chains at arrival; there is no separate join pass. Each home owns2^58 head words at[7*2^61,2^64): the head of index K2 mod2^58 is at7*2^61+8*index, formed by one BFI of K2's low58 bits into bits3..60 of the resident register x12 (bits61..63 set, bits0..2 clear; no other instruction writes x12), zeroed once by zero16384 before the first group, and one home record array of32-byte records below7*2^61 with a register-resident cursor and end. A record pointer p addresses the record's w2 word: its next word at p-16, K3' at p-8, (w2,w3) at p; heads, next words and list entries hold such pointers (zero ends a chain), and make_requests reads (w2,w3) at p. SWP (FEAT_LSE, like LDADD) is one load-and-store; after the zeroing only the receiver writes its heads and records. Records are appended in arrival order, so every accepted record is stored and inserted exactly once; records dropped at the full array are neither stored nor inserted.

Accepted path (12 including RET): the LD64B of the whole packet, which releases the window, and the DONE-bit branch (2); CMP/B.HS of the cursor against the last admissible position (2); a pre-indexed STP of (w2,w3) advancing the cursor by32 (1); the BFI head address (1); LSR and BFI forming K3', the packet's K3 with bits29..45 (fraction bits0..16, below the key) replaced by K2's bits58..63 and zeros (BFI width17) (2); an SWP that stores the new pointer into the head and returns the old head (1); an STP of (old head,K3') (1); CBZ on an empty chain and RET (2). Every record of a chain has the same K2 bits0..57 (its head index), so K3' equality is exactly agreement on K2 and the masked K3, i.e. on all175 key bits. Per visit: one LDP of the prior's (next,K3'), CMP, B.EQ and a CBZ/CBNZ on the next pointer (4); the walk alternates two pointer registers, so no move is needed. On a K3' match: an LDP of the prior's (w2,w3), two EORs with the new record's (w2,w3), ORR, TST of bits0..34 (mask0x7ffffffff: the source node (i,j)), CCMP of the list cursor against the list end when the sources differ, and B.HS back into the walk (7); on a match from another node with room the post-indexed STP of (prior,new) replaces the visit's pointer test, then RET; on a same-node match, or with the list full, the walk continues. A full-array drop costs5. The receive row is12 per potential record; walks are charged4 per visit and7 per K3' (full-key) match (section15); a listing adds nothing.

Same-index records form one arrival-order chain, newest first, every member reachable from the head: the new record is prepended (its next word is the old head) before the walk, which starts at the old head. The walk is uncapped and runs to the first prior from another source node agreeing on K2 and the masked K3 while the list has room, else to the chain end; chains are finite (one home's records, at most CAP_H), so every walk terminates on every run and no overflow event exists. Each new record is therefore linked to its most recent175-bit-agreeing predecessor from another source node (while the list has room), and the listed pairs are a subset of the cross-node175-bit-matching pairs at their home: exactly the pairs the candidate, request and relay rows (d_cl, d_rq, d_rl) bound (section6). The candidate list is cumulative with its2^29 cap checked at every attempted append; the final DONE marker of each group stores the list cursor to the receiver's list-end cell before the release STLR, and the main context loads it after acquiring the DONE cell, so the group's new candidates are exactly the entries between the previous and the published end. routing_check.py executes the receiver on the true layout (persistent resident registers, x12 checked after every call, heads at7*2^61, toy K2 below2^6 except top bits so distinct K2 share chains, planted cross-group and same-node same-key pairs, full home arrays and full lists): heads, next words, record words (K3' included), listed pairs, the published list end and record cursor, and the exact per-call count equal the path model, every head-following reaches exactly its index's members newest first, every listed pair joins two source nodes, same-node matches occur and are skipped, and while the list is not full each record is listed with exactly its latest earlier same-key record from another node.


# Section18: counted fold tails

Write delta=2^256-p. A fold carry implies its exact output z<9*delta<p (section3). Given H_g, each current Y is marginally uniform from its fresh chain start; retain the non-defective-batch indicator, without conditioning its distribution on cleanliness. For the forward fold z=R*(x(Y)-x1)*(x(Y)-x2) mod p: each z has at most two x roots and two curve points per root. For the backward fold z=R/(x(Y)-x2) mod p: each nonzero z has at most one x root and two points. Thus over the fewer-than9*delta output residues a carry has respectively at most36*delta and18*delta Y preimages. Uniformly given H_g, the two four-instruction tails cost at most216*delta/(r-1) per pair, hence FOLDMEAN=108*delta/(r-1) per incidence, by linearity alone. Identity X makes its batch defective and contributes zero to this valid-batch indicator. No residue-uniformity or independence heuristic enters this bound.

The ledger charges fold_tails=sum(PHI)*N*L*INC*FOLDMEAN+N*G*L*INC*4*d_batch: the defective part is unweighted at the full4 per incidence, exactly as section16. Both disclosed all-group bounds replace the row by4 per incidence on every batch. H_T still concerns only the original six Montgomery sites: the fold outputs, operands and labels are bit-identical. probe.py runs630 fold fixtures over every c=0..8, including both sides of every carry threshold, and checks congruence, exact old/new equality, the small-output implication and9/13 instructions including RET. The complete passes and group driver include both kinds of four-instruction tails in their executed counts. This finite evidence is not a production probability proof.
