The complete claim does not meet exploratory-v1. The reported 4,096-step invariant test and synthetic collision support local arithmetic/recovery, and the ledger's numerical sum is consistent, but material objections survive.

1. The instruction count does not justify the live attack. In the stated -DPW_FREESTANDING counting build, main is excluded. It is the only caller of static batch_inv, leaving the static INV array without a surviving writer. Thus step_close can be optimized using an identically zero inverse array, eliminating its actual field multiplications. The reported step_close tally—four mul instructions and zero umulh despite three field multiplications—is consistent with this defective counting setup, not the live initialized computation. The 423-instruction charge therefore lacks applicable support. The submitted bound has only about 0.05 instruction per walker-step of headroom above its ledger. Counting two back-substitution multiplications separately does not repair this discrepancy. The batch denominator scan and surrounding back-substitution overhead also lack a complete charge.

2. Production success is unsupported for this particular walk, not merely untested at full size. Alternating tables remove the described two-cycle but still admit four-cycles: with repeated jump rows A,B,A,B and canonicalization signs +,-,+,-, H goes to H+A, then -H-A-B, then -H-B, then H. This does not require equal jumps across tables. No cycle detection/escape or analysis of these traps over 2^111.63 steps per walker is supplied. The census reaches only 50,764 steps per walker. Its plateau and small heterogeneity statistic do not control this long-trail failure mode. The fitted saturation law extrapolates ltheta by 38 bits while changing curve, class-space size and walker count; matching Lambda alone does not justify that transfer. The CP calculation addresses sampling uncertainty under a common binomial rate, not this extrapolation error, and subtracting the observed 0.0124 residual supplies no supported production error bound. Consequently neither per-instance success 0.2297 nor amplified success 0.4066 is supported. Moreover, an exact full-point collision with distinct coefficients is useful for recovery, not a false positive rejected by verification, contrary to the asserted false-positive-only interpretation of record coincidences.

3. The stated production index is underspecified at its claimed capacity. Its eight-byte slots are explicitly priced as record pointers, but more than 2^65 records require more than 64 bits for unrestricted record identifiers, and larger byte addresses. No compressed or sharded addressing mechanism and corresponding work are supplied. The per-record calculation also uses successful-search probes although new-record insertion requires unsuccessful-search probes, whose larger expectation is acknowledged in the evidence.

The self-audit and certificate recompute submitted constants; they do not resolve these implementation, accounting and heuristic-support failures. Rejection does not require a production-sized execution or an unconditional cryptanalytic proof.
