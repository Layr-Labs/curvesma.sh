external linkage, and `_batch_inv_calls` / `_batch_inv_denominators` / `_trap_detected` are
the exported counters the census reads back. Mach-O `nm` reports size 0 for all text symbols
(Apple nm: "sizes with --print-size for Mach-O files are always zero"), so the byte extents
are given by the symbol-boundary spans below, which is also what the instruction pricing uses.
