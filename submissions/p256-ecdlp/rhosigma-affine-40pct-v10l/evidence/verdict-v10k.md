The complete original claim does not meet exploratory-v1. The reported small-curve recovery counts, 4,096-step invariant test and synthetic solve are relevant bounded evidence; production-scale execution is not required. However, concrete statistical, algorithmic and accounting flaws survive.

1. The claimed exact Clopper–Pearson lower endpoint is incorrect. cp95_lower solves Pr_p[X <= k-1] = 0.05, whereas the one-sided 95% lower endpoint requires 0.95. Its reported 0.252118 exceeds the observed 4942/20000 = 0.2471 and is not the stated lower endpoint. Subtracting an empirical residual does not repair this error. Thus the original statistical derivation of 0.2397 and 0.4220 is false as written, even if a different calculation might still exceed the minimum success threshold.

2. The production transfer lacks support for the supplied walk. The P-256 table generator chooses t = sp64(...) | 1, hence only 64-bit jump scalars, subsequently possibly negated modulo r. In contrast, the small-curve fixture uses jumps spanning its group order. For the supplied P-256 walk, after ell steps the scalar position has the form ±s0+d modulo r, with |d| < ell*2^64, approximately 2^175.63 at the declared trail length. This confinement is incompatible with simply applying the fixture's full-space collision model to independently uniform production starts. A full-order production jump generator or an argument overcoming this obstruction is absent. Matching Lambda, fitting saturation across nine cells and observing small heterogeneity do not establish the necessary production mixing assumptions; ltheta itself is extrapolated, not matched.

3. The work ledger contradicts the source. step_forward computes the prefix products, and the subsequently called batch_inv resets PRE[0] and recomputes every prefix product. The claimed removal of this duplicate multiplication has not occurred. One additional field multiplication per walker-step already exceeds the submitted bound's very small rounding slack under the submission's own prices. Moreover, the stated PW_FREESTANDING counting build excludes main, leaving batch_inv without a caller and static INV unwritten. Optimization can therefore remove arithmetic that would be needed with actual inverses. The reported step_close count of only four scalar multiplications and zero UMULH instructions does not justify its real three-field-multiplication path. The arithmetic consistency certificate does not validate these missing execution costs.

4. The implementation's distinguished-point predicate masks 61 bits, not the declared 62. Under the submission's own density heuristic this doubles the record count, raising record storage from about 2^72.332 to 2^73.332 bytes, above the claimed 2^73-byte cap. The stated sorted-shard price also accounts for binary search without justifying the cost of maintaining sorted order: a constant insertion/shift allowance is insufficient for an ordinary sorted array, and the supplied processor instead implements a different fixed-size hash table.

These are failures of the original success, implementation and resource claims, not merely residual scientific uncertainty. The self-audit and certificate check consistency of asserted inputs but do not resolve them; neither the complete work bound nor the claimed production success and storage bounds is adequately supported.
