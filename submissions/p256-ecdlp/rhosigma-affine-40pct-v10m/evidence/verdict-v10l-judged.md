# submission cbc03d46 — status rejected (verbatim)

The complete original claim fails exploratory-v1. The revised source addresses the dead inverse-writer and duplicate-prefix issues, and the corrected CP lower-endpoint equation is appropriate. Reported local tests are relevant bounded evidence. However, concrete implementation, success and accounting flaws survive.

1. The claimed four-step detector and escape are not implemented. step_close compares x3 with W[i].x4, then assigns the current W[i].x to x4. After initialization this compares points two steps apart, not four; there is no four-entry history ring. A match only increments trap_detected: it does not restart, escape or change the transition. Thus the claimed priced period-four escape with latency four is false as shipped. The separate fixture census cannot supply this missing mechanism. Its repeated-state key also omits the alternating-table phase, so a repeated key need not imply a permanent cycle.

2. Production success has a concrete obstruction beyond finite experimental scope. recproc_init still generates each jump from a single 64-bit t, with only possible negation modulo the group order. Consequently a trail's scalar position has the form ±s0+d with |d| < ell*2^64, approximately 2^175.63 at the declared length. With 65,536 independently uniform starts in a roughly 2^256-order group, overlap between these confined regions is negligible, contrary to the full-space collision model used to justify success 0.2297. The small-curve tables span their group instead. Reading only a coordinate limb does not remove this difference, and no alternative production jump generator or applicable success argument is supplied.

3. The fitted recovery census also tests the old, unguarded transition, not the new previous-row-dependent walk. Matching points with different guard registers need not coalesce. Neither matching Lambda nor the fitted plateau establishes the new walk's production recovery probability. XOR is a bijection for a fixed previous row, but this alone does not prove conditional uniformity along a correlated walk. The fit file gives a production limit of 0.24212, not the claimed derivation of 0.2297, and includes residuals around 0.020 rather than the asserted 0.0095. Correct CP calibration addresses binomial sampling uncertainty, not these transition and extrapolation errors. Hence the amplified success 0.4066 is unsupported. Exact full-point collisions with distinct coefficients are useful recovery events, not inherently false positives rejected by verification.

4. The purported 66-bit addressing repair truncates addresses. rec_addr performs the composition and multiplication in u64; for example, rec_addr(0,0) and rec_addr(2^30,0) both return zero. The nominal storage totals may fit the cap, but this function does not implement the claimed address space, and the supplied fixed-size hash table does not resolve the production addressing requirement.

5. The exact work bound is not justified even by the submitted ledger: it gives log2(W)=128.8794460432104, exceeding the claimed at-most exponent 128.8794. The certificate's claim_covers_W=true contradicts those numbers; rounding downward cannot establish the bound. Furthermore, fe_inv performs 256 squarings, 128 conditional multiplications and one final correction multiplication, whereas the ledger charges 384 rather than 385 field multiplications per inversion. No justified tighter complete calculation repairs the submitted bound.

These are surviving flaws in the original claims, not a demand for unconditional proof or production-scale execution.
