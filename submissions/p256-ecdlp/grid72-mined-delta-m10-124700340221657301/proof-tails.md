# Section16: correction tails and the disclosed heuristic H_T
Sites. Each X-pair has six data-dependent correction tails in the leaf passes, three per incidence; the fold tails are separate in section18. The canonical d2 correction is a separate four-instruction branch, not one of these six REDC tails. It is charged on every X-pair in both the expected row and the every-run bound. A half-rate credit is not used, because the group-execution event conditions on the fixed X. All six REDC sites are carry-only tails (B.CC over four): forward A_j=M(A_(j-1),e_j); backward inv=M(A_(j-1),B), B<-M(e_j,B), i1=M(d2,inv), and the squares w=S(i1), w=S(i2). The worst case 3*4=12 per incidence sits on top of the long path. With no REDC tail taken, a long-path incidence costs4331/8=541.375 before fold tails: 152 forward and3723/4 backward per X-pair. The backward figure deletes both stride MOVs and adds one end-pointer reload per four pairs. The per-Y kernels (chain step, Y-entry preparation) have twelve tail sites per Y; they are charged in full in the1836 per Y and use no heuristic. The per-run worst case keeps all tails and the four-instruction borrow.
E-row. Split group g's tail excess F_g=F_g^nd+F_g^d over its non-defective and defective batches. Group g's tail work is executed only if T>g-1, an event fixed by H_g (section15), so E[1[T>g-1]F_g^nd]<=PHI[g]*max E[F_g^nd|H_g]: given H_g the group's batches use fresh chain coins (c,s), and H_T bounds the conditional rate of each tail on a non-defective batch (linearity only). The defective part is charged without PHI at the worst12 per incidence: E[F^d]<=12*2^20*E[defective batches]<=12*2^20*N*G*L*d_batch, with the unconditional d_batch of section6 (its unused identity-X term included). flip_ledger.py: correction_tails=sum_g PHI[g]*N*L*2^20*3.5+N*G*L*2^20*12*d_batch, first_prefix_tail=sum_g PHI[g]*N*L*8. The PHI=1 bound and the per-run worst case keep every tail on every batch.
H_T (the one heuristic): for a non-defective batch, given the history H_g before its group and over the group's fresh chain coins, the six tail sites of each X-pair have total expected excess at most3.5 instructions per incidence (four instructions per taken tail; six sites per pair, two incidences per pair); the charge is the expectation of a sum, so by linearity it is fixed by the per-site rates, not by their maximum, and charging the largest site rate to all six sites (4.2) is a valid but looser site-max bound, except two structurally special positions per batch, both charged again in full (4 each, first_prefix_tail): the first forward multiply A_0=M(R,e_0), whose multiplicand is the Montgomery one R=2^256-p<2^224, and the final backward update B_(-1)=M(e_0,B_0), which represents2^64 now that B is scaled; its tail was taken in no census batch. Support: (1) the Montgomery extra-reduction model (Schindler 2000; Walter-Thompson 2001): with m uniform and operands behaving as uniform residues, P(T>=R0) is about E[ab]/(p*R0): about1/4 for two full operands and1/3 for the square of a full operand; the folded table sums e, i2 and the canonical d2 are full residues below 2^256. (2) probe.py runs the exact integer tail model of all six sites (the model checked call by call against the executed listing: executed count minus four times the taken REDC/fold tails plus four per non-borrow d2 equals the worst-d2 formula on every call) on random P-256 points: 32 batches of 4 Y's by 16 pairs at seed97 give site rates0.2300 (forward),0.2505 (inv),0.2383 (B),0.2671 (i1),0.3140 and0.3315 (squares), mean excess3.26 per incidence against the charged3.5; the final update and the first prefix multiply have rate0.0. All figures in (2) to (4) are for this package's stream (DT step, B scaled, mined X pairs with0<x2-x1<2^64). (3) A per-position census of the same model, run outside the hosted experiment with the shipped code (`python3 -c "import flip_check as fc; print(fc.census(SEED, 3000))"` for SEED 1 and 2, 3000 batches of 64 pair positions each) found every site at every position at most0.354; inv, the first prefix multiply (their multiplicand is the small Montgomery one) and the final update at position0 are at0.0; binomial standard error about0.009. (4) Under H_g the X's and their tables are fixed and only the Y's are fresh: with the X set held fixed (fc.census(...,fixed_x=True)), 8 X sets of 3000 batches each, run outside the hosted experiment, give every site at most0.3357, every position at most0.367 and mean excess at most3.3155 against the charged3.5; probe.py repeats a small fixed-X census (4 X sets of16 batches) and reports its rates without a threshold: at that size the per-site standard error is about0.015, so it illustrates the model rather than testing the envelope, and only one structural fact (first prefix multiply not taken) is asserted. Limitations: H_T is unproved (representatives are deterministic functions of the points); the census is finite. H_T enters only the expected-work bound; success (section6) and the per-run worst case use no tail assumption.
# Section17: independent gate for the changed run
Before startup the coordinator reads a fresh64-bit RNDR u. If u>=K, it returns0 at charged cost64; all other nodes remain idle. Otherwise startup and every worker's work are included in E_run. With the exact rational success s_run computed by section6, K=7196808566734747396 is the least integer satisfying(K/2^64)*s_run>=0.39; the checker asserts both K and K-1. The decimal lower bound0.9996418852272583244074 also suffices. This K is recomputed because the success accounting (the home cap with its holes, the caps) and the run geometry changed.
Pointwise work=64+1[u<K]*work_run, hence E=64+(K/2^64)*E_run. A closed gate is charged and counted as a failure. All cold setup follows the gate. G=1600,L=67308000 is a concrete evaluated geometry.
# Section12 receiver: block join and the next array
The receiver (route.s rblk) joins every accepted record into its home's hash chains as its block arrives; there is no separate join pass. Each home owns2^58 head words at[7*2^61,2^64) (the head of index K2 mod2^58 at7*2^61+8*index, formed by one BFI of K2's low58 bits into bits3..60 of the resident x12; zeroed once by zero16384 before the first group), one record array of32-byte records [K2,K3,w2,w3] whose cursor x10 is32 mod64 at every block start, and a next array with one16-byte entry (next, K2) per record slot: record k at RB+32k has entry NB+16k, so record=C+2*entry with the resident constant C=RB-2*NB. Heads and next words hold entry addresses (zero ends a chain); list entries hold the addresses of the two records' w2 words, where make_requests reads (w2,w3) and, below them, K2 and K3 (it forms K3': K3 with bits28..44, fraction bits0..16 below the key, replaced by K2's bits58..63 and zeros). SWP (FEAT_LSE, like LDADD) is one load-and-store; after the zeroing only the receiver writes its heads, records and entries.
A block is a header (destination with the DONE flag in bit63, the sender's fixed window, count c<=128) on the receiver's port. rblk reads it (3) and tests the room (4): if x10+32c+32 exceeds the array end the whole block is dropped and nothing is stored. Otherwise it copies and joins: the first record by LDP/STP (window and cursor are32 mod64), then floor((c-1)/2) pairs, each one LD64B from the window and one ST64B into the array (4 a pair), in chunks of1,2,4,8,16 and32 pairs selected by six TBZ, then a last single record if c is even; if c is odd the cursor skips one32-byte hole and its entry, so the next block is aligned again. Each record is joined as it is copied (4): BFI of the head address, SWP of the entry cursor into the head, post-indexed STP of (old head, K2) into the entry, CBZ on an empty chain. Then one STLR acknowledges the block (the sender may reuse its window), and a DONE block increments the done count: non-final3 more, final6 more (STP of the list cursor and record cursor to the list-end cell, STLR of1 to the DONE cell, count reset). A full block costs6 per record plus27; any block at most6c+35.
Walk. Same-index records form one arrival-order chain, newest first, every member reachable from the head: the new record is prepended before its walk, which starts at the old head and is inline. A visit is the LDP of the prior's (next, K2), CMP with the new K2, B.NE and the CBZ/CBNZ on the next entry (4); two pointer registers alternate, so no move is needed. On a K2 hit (all64 bits; the chain fixes58) a short routine is called: it loads the prior's K3 and compares it under the mask0xffffe0000fffffff (7 with BL and RET if it differs: no full-key match); on a full175-bit match it loads the prior's (w2,w3), tests the source node (bits0..35 of both words) and the list end by CCMP (14 if the walk continues: same node, or list full), and otherwise appends the pair and ends the walk (18). The walk is uncapped and runs to the first prior from another source node agreeing on all175 key bits while the list has room, else to the chain end; chains are finite (one home's records, at most CAP_H), so every walk terminates on every run and no overflow event exists. Each new record is therefore linked to its most recent175-bit-agreeing predecessor from another source node (while the list has room), and the listed pairs are a subset of the cross-node175-bit-matching pairs at their home: exactly the pairs the candidate, request and relay rows (d_cl, d_rq, d_rl) bound (section6). The ledger charges4 per visit,7 per K2 hit and18 per full-key match (section15). The candidate list is cumulative with its2^27 cap checked at every append; the final DONE block of each group stores the list cursor to the list-end cell before the release STLR, and the main context loads it after acquiring the DONE cell, so the group's new candidates are exactly the entries between the previous and the published end. routing_check.py executes rblk on the true layout against an independent model of every stored word and the exact per-call count (RECEIVER_PROTOCOL section4).
# Section18: counted fold tails
Write delta=2^256-p. A fold carry implies its exact output z<9*delta<p (section3). Given H_g, each current Y is marginally uniform from its fresh chain start; retain the non-defective-batch indicator, without conditioning its distribution on cleanliness. For the forward fold z=R*(x(Y)-x1)*(x(Y)-x2) mod p: each z has at most two x roots and two curve points per root. For the backward fold z=R/(x(Y)-x2) mod p: each nonzero z has at most one x root and two points. Thus over the fewer-than9*delta output residues a carry has respectively at most36*delta and18*delta Y preimages. Uniformly given H_g, the two four-instruction tails cost at most216*delta/(r-1) per pair, hence FOLDMEAN=108*delta/(r-1) per incidence, by linearity alone. A mined X is never the identity. No residue-uniformity or independence heuristic enters this bound.
The ledger charges fold_tails=sum(PHI)*N*L*INC*FOLDMEAN+N*G*L*INC*4*d_batch: the defective part is unweighted at the full4 per incidence, exactly as section16. Both disclosed all-group bounds replace the row by4 per incidence on every batch. H_T concerns only the six Montgomery sites. The DT step's sum is folded by the same fold with carry at most2, so a fold carry again implies z<9*delta and the backward bound above holds for it verbatim; its output is congruent to the former lookup's but can be another representative below2^256, so Section16's census is rerun on this stream with mined X pairs. probe.py runs630 fold fixtures over every c=0..8, including both sides of every carry threshold, and checks congruence, exact old/new equality, the small-output implication and9/13 instructions including RET. The complete passes and group driver include both kinds of four-instruction tails in their executed counts. This finite evidence is not a production probability proof.
## H_T successor scope: the aggregate3.5 charge
This revision charges the per-site mean rather than the site maximum: TAILE=3.5 (F(7,2)). The six-site expectation is linear in the per-site rates, so the sum of site averages (observed mean excess3.2928..3.3319) is the correct object; the earlier4.2 applied the largest site average to all six sites. The canonical d2 branch adds p exactly when the subtraction borrows; the long-path count includes its four instructions on every pair.
The seed 20261105 probe passes. A separate census of this stream repeats seeds 1,2 with fresh mined X pairs and seeds 0..7 with fixed X, 300 batches each, 64 positions. Fixed-X maximum site-average is 0.3398 (fresh 0.3339; the 3000-batch runs of (3), (4) give 0.3342 and 0.3357); maximum position rate is 0.4233; mean excess ranges 3.2928..3.3319. The 0.35 envelope bounds every site average, with margin above the 0.3398 empirical maximum and the 0.3333 Montgomery squaring model, and the charge is 3.5 instructions per incidence (0.168 above the largest observed mean excess 3.3319, and 0.167 above the 10/3 type-aware Montgomery mean). B[0]=F[0]=I[0]=0 in all ten censuses; the two special positions remain fully charged. Neither site averages nor a binomial interval on pooled observations imply a uniform history-conditional law. Fixed-X iid Y sampling omits long-chain and stopping/cleanliness conditioning. The production3.5 conditional envelope is a plausible, unproved extrapolation from the Montgomery model and finite data, not inherited validity or a confidence theorem. Only its expected nondefect row changes; success and every-run bounds remain independent of H_T.
