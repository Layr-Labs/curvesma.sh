### 2.1 Removal of the Tier-2 fallback (the rejected volume step) and the relation-conditioned Band-3 loss

The previous package ran a Tier-2 `M_w`-only enumeration on every Tier-1 gate failure and charged it `<= 440` Ir/hit via an LLL determinant inequality plus an ellipsoid-volume count. The latter step was the rejected gap: the LLL inequality controls products of Gram–Schmidt lengths, not the number of integer points in a projected ellipsoid. A Blichfeldt–van der Corput count would need an upper bound on `vol(K + (1/2)(F-F))`; Brunn–Minkowski only *lower*-bounds that mixed volume, so it cannot certify the integer-point bound. Rather than ship an invalid count, this package removes the fallback. A Tier-1 gate failure aborts the orbit; no integer enumeration, no volume argument and no counting theorem is used or charged.

**Relation-conditioned Band-3 loss lemma (replaces the flat `1 - p_fb` charge).** The H2 sub-premise bounds the *hit-weighted* fraction of Band-3 sieve hits that belong to aborted orbits: `p_fb <= 0.005`. That is a statement about **hits**, not about relations, and one relation carries several Band-3 factors. Under the package's own norm/smoothness model (the same `Retention(lg, lx2, prime_min = 2^14, explicit_bits = 14, step = 0.25, max_bits = 530, tilt = 0.05)` object that produces `r_g`), a usable `g`-norm near `2^470.2` (`470.14` mixture mean, `470.29` relation-weighted mean) carries

    E[k_B3 | usable] = int_{54.5}^{60} phi(b) db/b = 2.1999

prime factors in `[2^54.5, 2^60)`, where `phi(b) = p * P(p | g-norm usable) = Num(n-b)/Num(n)` is the model's own usable-relation density ratio (`Num` is the retention numerator, so `phi` is evaluated from `Retention.at` and `Retention.denominator`, not from a second model). At the mixture-mean norm `n = 470.14`, `phi(2^54.5) = 19.738` and `phi(2^60) = 26.452`; `phi(n,b)` increases in both `n` and `b`, so the charged factor is the **supremum over the 35 discrete norm sizes actually present in the relation-weighted pool mixture and over the band**:

    phi_max = sup_{n in supp(mix), b in [54.5,60]} phi(n,b) = phi(475.75, 60.0) = 26.88771383465995.

No averaging over norm size enters `phi_max`; the mixture-mean value `26.452` is quoted only as a cross-check. Charging `1 - p_fb` once per relation therefore under-charges by ~2.2x, and the fixed factor is replaced by the following bound.

*Derivation.* (i) A usable relation is lost iff at least one of its Band-3 prime factors has its supporting orbit aborted: an aborted orbit emits no bucket entries, the position keeps an un-sieved cofactor `p`, and the stated Stage-B window (`sb = 55`, 0-LP) rejects any un-sieved cofactor `> 2^55`. Band-3 factors in `[2^54.5, 2^55)` would still pass Stage B and could be recovered by the 0-LP split, so charging them as lost is conservative. The Band-3 sieve is a `g`-ideal `tau`-orbit sieve: its hit count `hits_b3 = ln(60/54.5)` is charged once per cell against the `g`-norm, and the `f`-norm is cofactorized in the Stage-B split (`C_NORM_F`, the 7-limb `f`-SRT and the `q_f` ECM batch, all in the `split` row), not sieved — so aborted orbits remove `g`-side factors only. (An uncharged sensitivity: applying the same union bound to the `f`-norm distribution would add `0.010126`, combined loss `0.022714`, i.e. a claim of `2^85.466528` — above the bar — if the `f`-side were sieved by these orbits; it is not.) (ii) With `k_p` the number of Band-3 prime factors of the `g`-norm, the union bound over the relation's own factors gives `P(lost | usable) <= sum_{p in aborted} P(p | usable) = sum_{p in aborted} phi(p)/p`; no independence of the aborted set from a relation's factor set is assumed; the supremum over the declared norm-size support and the band covers adversarial placement of the aborted hit mass. (iii) The H2 hit mass of the band is `sum_{p in [2^54.5,2^60)} 1/p = ln(60/54.5) = 0.0961439` (the model's own `hits_b3`), and the H2 premise bounds the aborted part of it by `p_fb`, so `sum_{p in aborted} 1/p <= p_fb * 0.0961439` and

    loss <= phi_max * p_fb * ln(60/54.5) = 26.88771383465995 * 0.005 * 0.096143860553 = 0.0129254430.

The `phi_max` is the supremum over the 35 norm sizes in the declared mixture and the whole band, so the only model inputs are H2's hit-weighted `p_fb` and the package's own retention/norm-size model H1; no independence is assumed. The mixture-average variant (`phi = 26.452105661225`, loss `0.0127160378`, claim `2^85.449866188`) is quoted as a check, not charged; the shipped claim uses the supremum `26.88771383465995`. As a further sensitivity, adding the prime-harmonic correction `1/ln^2(2^54.5) = 7.0e-4` to the band mass gives loss `0.0130196` and claim `2^85.450355049`, still below the bar.

*Cross-check (not charged).* The same model's per-factor thinning expectation, `E[(1-p_fb)^{k_B3}] = 0.989048` (loss `0.010952`), confirms that the charged union bound is the conservative one; at `z = 1` the marked series reproduces `Retention.at` to `2e-15` (audit check `band3_relation_loss_ok`, which recomputes `phi_max`, the loss and this cross-check independently of `compute_ledger`).
