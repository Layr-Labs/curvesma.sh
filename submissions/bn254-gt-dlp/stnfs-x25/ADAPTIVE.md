# Recursive reached-ideal evidence

The preregistered shuffled-order campaign contains24 fixed roots (12f at20bits and12g at17bits),20root successes and4explicit censors,145fresh entered nodes, maximum depth4. Four same-root deterministic-order runs (f0,f1,g0,g1) contain3successes,1censor and26entered nodes. Every entered node, including those inside censored runs, remains in the PGF table; censoring is not counted as mathematical failure or silently removed. Soft15second/node12 budgets bound these diagnostics.

The actual reduced tower is over the production BN base, with f_t=x^4+w*x^2+w^2,g_t=x^2+4w. Complete radius2 balls are enumerated; finite windows are factored exactly and degree-one child roots checked. Child windows strictly shrink. All ideals<=2^12 are treated known; this is a reduced model, not a production factor base. Repeated ideals use a toy cache, while production aborts repeats. The24stress runs shuffle distinct qualifying relation order; the four aligned runs use the recorded deterministic witness order. The four aligned runs do not assert that every production unit-orbit duplicate or traversal detail has been modeled.

At each node, M is the actual qualifying distinct-relation count and raw mu is the same toy model scale used before seeing descendants. Compare mean t^M to mean (1+0.5*mu*(1-t)/shape)^(-shape) on t=0,.01,...,.8. Pre-entry strata preserve depth and already-known failure/sibling history. All recorded strata pass, including11nodes after an earlier failed relation (worst ratio about.802),26after any completed node failure (about.911), and41after a successful sibling (about.9756, close to1). These subsets are small, correlated and overlapping; they are descriptive pointwise comparisons, not confidence bounds. No post-hoc observations are discarded and no parameter is tuned to make these comparisons pass.

These runs directly exercise multilevel adaptive relation resolution and provide stronger scoped evidence than pooled counts and one-level child/control averages. They do not prove a uniform production conditional law, a fixed-ideal success theorem, independence, or production descendant-solvability transfer. H-PGF and its conditional next-relation/child transfer remain explicit extrapolation hypotheses. The conservative half-scale envelope, full cofactor charging, node/repeat bounds and meter losses are retained. No exact negative-binomial mean or universal positivity is introduced.

Files in libextra.b85 retain all28 raw result records, all171 count/history rows and their replay/source helpers. part=adaptive_stats recomputes strata; part=adaptive with side,root_seed and order regenerates a bounded root. Recorded deterministic evidence uses seeds0/1; timing censors can vary. Scales are floored to0.001 in the compact table. All earlier862 observations and toy/child replays remain available.

Joint descendant outcomes are retained alongside PGF counts. Shuffled-order relation attempts contain131successes,7genuine failures and7censors. After an earlier failed relation, the recorded attempts are5successes,0genuine failures and2censors: these unknown outcomes permit an unresolved failure fraction up to2/7, not zero risk. All8genuine failed nodes in the combined campaign have M=0; no observed M>0 node exhausted all descendants. These are coarsened, adaptively reached toy ensembles with small dependent subgroups. They measure actual recursive descendant resolution, but cannot establish uniform production solvability or conditional independence. The replay reports joint tables by depth and earlier failure with censors as unknown outcome intervals.

These observations are used only at their scoped empirical strength. The corrected count-tail plus sequential-availability proof is analytic and does not infer joint domination from these data.
