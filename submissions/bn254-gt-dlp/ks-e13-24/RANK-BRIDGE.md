# Finite relation rank and padded block Wiedemann

This section states two different premises. They must not be conflated with class-number and unit-map assumptions.

**H-R (finite relation spanning).** For the actually collected, tau-quotiented and filtered relation matrix A=[M|S] over F_r, with D=N+7, M has column rank N and A has rank D-1. Its kernel is exactly the line generated by the virtual logarithms (w,c), with c!=0. This is a structural NFS relation-saturation heuristic for this fixed finite relation set. Counting smooth relations, class numbers prime to r, and injective Schirokauer maps do not prove it. The excess, complete relation-generation rules, occurrence/filtering experiments and standard finite-field NFS framework provide its exploratory support. Synthetic planted matrices and solver replays do not establish saturation of the production relation set. If H-R fails, randomized Wiedemann cannot repair the missing relation information; rank/verification checks can reject but do not supply a failure-probability bound.

Write B=[M|0], a D-by-D matrix. Then ImB=ImM has dimension N, and V=ImB+span(S) has dimension D-1 under H-R. Use Y=[S_1,...,S_7,BZ], with n-7 independently random columns Z. Fix the coordinate partition publicly before sampling weights: the seven pad coordinates each belong to a distinct projection block; nonpad coordinates use the specified fixed shuffled/balanced partition into m blocks. X has exactly one independent uniform 64-bit entry (low 64 bits of a 128-bit draw; zero included) on each coordinate's block, with zeros elsewhere. Z entries are independent uniform 64-bit values, zero included.

**H-X (finite block controllability/observability).** At the finite right/left lengths d,e used by the solver, assume two specific minors are nonzero polynomials: an N-by-N minor of

    C=[BZ, BY, B^2Y, ..., B^dY]

and a (D-1)-by-(D-1) minor of O U, where O stacks X^T,X^TB,...,X^TB^(e-1) and U is a fixed basis of V, determined by the fixed relation matrix, not by X or Z. This includes generator-length and sparse-projection applicability hypotheses; it is not inferred merely from rankM=N. The lexicographically first minor having a nonzero polynomial can define the selected indices for analysis; the attack need not compute a symbolic determinant. Without H-X, e.g. a large repeated-eigenvalue matrix can have full rank M yet inadequate finite block Krylov span.

The control minor has degree at most N in Z; the observation minor has degree at most D-1=N+6 in X. Their nonzero product has total degree at most2N+6<4N. Schwartz-Zippel on the independently sampled entries, each on at least2^64 field values (X and Z 2^64), therefore gives simultaneous control/observation failure at most4N/2^64, CONDITIONAL on H-R and H-X. This is not a bound on the probability that H-R/H-X themselves hold.

When the minors are nonzero, C spans ImB and O is injective on V. Since the true c!=0 satisfies Sc in ImB, there is a finite polynomial relation sum_i B^iY f_i=0 whose first seven constant coefficients are nonzero as a vector. Projected and full vector relations coincide on V. Use the already-priced fast block minimal-generator/approximant-basis algorithm to obtain a basis of the projected relation module in the stated finite degree window; then select a basis column of degree at most d whose first seven constant coordinates are not all zero, or fail if none exists. The production attack does not materialize the dense Hankel matrix or perform a dense Gaussian solve; the small replay uses Gaussian elimination only as an independent algebra diagnostic. Do not fix the first coefficient to1: the true first map coefficient might be zero. The same selection is applied to the standard polynomial-generator basis: a relation with nonzero constant S-part implies some generator basis column has such a part, because polynomial linear combinations obtain their constant term from constant basis columns.

For f_0=(c,e), Y f_0=Sc+BZ e. Define

    wbar=Z e+sum_(i>=1) B^(i-1)Y f_i.

Then M wbar_firstN+Sc=0. Thus (wbar_firstN,c) is nonzero and belongs to kerA, hence is proportional to the virtual-log vector under H-R. Evaluate it by commuted Horner (lib commute.txt), reconstruct purged logs and verify the final target equation.

The block lengths are ceil(D/n)+1 and ceil(D/m)+1 for the bounded diagnostic and may be used in the attack. Rounding/endpoints add at most five block steps beyond the leading N/n+N/m accounting; at the protected N<2^45.72,n=40 and per-row-vector price<2^12, these steps cost<2^65.5 scalar instructions. This fits the unused part of the existing2^68 wrapper allowance after its field-map bound<2^67. The corresponding O(1) extra generator terms are also below that allowance. No new quadratic leading work or changed normalization is assumed.

The seven-pad replay uses synthetic C4-filtered coefficient rows and PLANTED Schirokauer columns/log vector to establish its premises by construction, then independently checks ranks, finite block generation/observation, a projected relation with nonzero S-constant part, Horner reconstruction and the final kernel equation. A distinct-eigenvalue diagonal family supplies another explicit witness where the polynomial is nonzero. A control collapsing all pads into one projection block must fail observation. These are algebra/implementation diagnostics, not evidence that production NFS relations span the needed space. H-R and production H-X remain explicit empirical/structural extrapolations under exploratory review.

## Primary framework and scope

Schirokauer, *The impact of the number field sieve on the discrete logarithm problem in finite fields*, §2.2 Step4, constructs valuation/character vectors and explicitly enlarges the relation region when the desired vector is outside their span. This separates solvability from the character-map argument. This package fixes its finite region and therefore states H-R for that actual set; the source does not prove H-R for BN254. [Author framework](https://library.slmath.org/books/Book44/files/12oliver.pdf).

Fried, Gaudry, Heninger and Thome, *A kilobit hidden SNFS discrete logarithm computation*, §3.1, separates filtering, Schirokauer columns, the homogeneous (M|S) system and virtual logarithms. This supports the algebraic framework and its practical use, not saturation of this different fixed tower/quotient. [Primary computation](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1610.02874). The actual application to this finite structured BN254 relation set remains a declared heuristic, supported by relation excess and occurrence/filtering diagnostics; no synthetic random-matrix probability is substituted for it. H-R/H-X are deterministic applicability hypotheses here, not independently quantified random losses; the4N/2^64 allowance is conditional on them.

Seven-pad diagnostics: diagonal seeds1/2 have N29,D36,M/C rank29,A/O/H rank35; seed2 tests a true zero first map coefficient. The filtered synthetic case (B = 2^58 profile) has N128,D135,M/C rank128,A/O/H rank134. Collapsing all pads into one projection block gives O/H rank30<35, the expected negative control. ## H-X: structure of B and production-scale evidence

Structure (proved here; no heuristic). Write M = [M1; M2], M1 its first N rows. Then det(xI - B) = x^7 det(xI - M1), and for lambda != 0, Bv = lambda v iff v = (v1, M2 v1 / lambda) with M1 v1 = lambda v1: B and the N-by-N block M1 have the same nonzero eigenvalues with the same geometric multiplicities. Since M has column rank N (H-R), ker B = {v : v_1 = ... = v_N = 0} has dimension 7, so on Im B the eigenvalue 0 has geometric multiplicity at most 7: the zero pad columns cannot create a repeated eigenvalue wider than the block.

Genuine NFS matrices. This construction (iterate on (M|0), starting block Y = (S | random block), a very low-weight projection X, the solution read from one column of the minimal generator) is the one Fried, Gaudry, Heninger and Thome ran in CADO-NFS for their 1024-bit SNFS discrete logarithm (arXiv 1610.02874, section 5.3): a genuine filtered relation matrix of 28 million rows with 3 Schirokauer columns, blocking factors (m, n) = (24, 12), sequence length about N/m + N/n; the computation obtained the logarithms of 198M elements. CADO-NFS's prep step checks the rank conditions on the first iterates before the sequence. That run is a production-scale, unplanted instance of the finite block controllability and observability H-X states, with 9 random starting columns against our 33 and 24 projections against our 1,024. It is evidence for filtered NFS matrices, not a proof for this tower: H-X remains a declared heuristic.
