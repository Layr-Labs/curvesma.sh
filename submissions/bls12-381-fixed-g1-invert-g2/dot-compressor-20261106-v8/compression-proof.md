# Injective norm-one encoding and its charged computation

Write X=a+bw in Fp12=Fp6[w]/(w²-v), with a,b in Fp6. The protected field has degree12 and its quadratic conjugation fixes Fp6 and sends w to-w; it equals the p^6 Frobenius. Direct modular exponentiation of the protected public p and r checks p^6=-1 mod r. Hence every GT element has X^(p^6+1)=1, so a²-vb²=1. This uses the entire protected GT, including identity, without assuming a subgroup of reduced order.

Given b, the equation a²=1+vb² has at most two roots in the odd-characteristic field Fp6. They are a and-a. Store b's six canonical Montgomery coefficients (48 bytes each) and one byte s. Set s=1 if the six-coefficient unsigned lexicographic tuple for a is greater than the tuple for-a, otherwise s=0. The comparison uses a fixed coefficient/limb/byte order. If a=0 the only root is zero and s=0; otherwise the tuples are different and their comparison reverses under a->-a. Thus (b,s) distinguishes every GT element injectively. Its size is289 bytes. No square root or decompression is needed to hash a stored full element, and full576-byte states remain in the jump table and checkpoint registers. Both elements with the same b and opposite a are distinguished, even if both could occur. Equality testing compares the injective keys as proved in checkpoint-proof.md.

Compute the sign without explicitly constructing -a. Use the same fixed coefficient order and compare coefficients as unsigned canonical Montgomery integers. If a coefficient is zero, its negation is zero and that tuple position agrees. At the first nonzero coefficient x, canonical negation is p-x. Since p is odd, x>p-x is equivalent to x>floor(p/2), and equality is impossible. If every coefficient is zero, the sign is zero. Therefore the new computation returns exactly the inherited sign for every full-production group element; no walk label, probability argument or encoding changes.

Each Fp coefficient occupies six 64-bit limbs. Fully unroll six coefficient tests: six LDRs, five ORRs and a CBNZ to that coefficient's comparison label. This is 12 instructions per tested coefficient, at most 72. Keep the six loaded coefficient limbs live in x1..x6. At the selected label, load the six limbs of H=(p+1)/2 from a constant table, compare with one SUBS followed by five SBCS from least significant limb upward, and CSET CS the sign using the final no-borrow flag. Since x>=H is equivalent to x>floor(p/2), this gives exactly the inherited sign. Six loads, six subtracts and one CSET CS suffice, 13 instructions. Both the zero path and selected path store exactly one sign byte and return. Constants are prepared during charged setup.

Use x0 for the input base, x1..x6 for limbs, x7 for OR accumulation, x8 for the constant-table base, x9 for constant limbs, x10 for subtraction scratch and x11 for output. No callee-saved registers or calls are needed. Every state offset is fixed below 288 bytes, and constant offsets are below 48. Entry pointer preparation, optional caller BL, branch to return, sign-byte store and RET fit within 32 additional instructions. Thus at most 72+13+32=117 instructions execute. Charge 128, including caller allowance for caller preparation and any ABI moves. b serialization and hash preparation remain in the separate unchanged SHA 2048 preparation allowance. No square root, inversion or modular negation is performed.
