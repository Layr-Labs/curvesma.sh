# Full standard hashes and the sole cryptographic bridge

Premise H is explicit:the actual full standard unkeyed BLAKE3-256 evaluations in the stated distinct domains behave as independent uniform outputs at this attack's full query scale. This is a cryptographic heuristic,not a theorem about the fixed function. Its support is the full standard seven-round construction and separated domain/messages;the extrapolation to roughly2^126 queries remains material uncertainty. No reduced-round hash is substituted. No other random-mapping,independent collision or mixing heuristic is used.

The native key S192 is fingerprinted to U40 using five independent NH128 lanes. For each lane sum twelve products((word2i+key2i)mod2^64)*((word2i+1+key2i+1)mod2^64) modulo2^128. Use exact24 native64-bit key words. NH's same-length full128-bit output collision bound is2^-64 (Black/Halevi/Krawczyk/Krovetz/Rogaway,UMAC1999,Theorem4.2,https://www.cs.ucdavis.edu/~rogaway/umac/umac_full.pdf). Independently multiply each lane by a uniform odd128-bit A modulo2^128 and take high64 bits. For x=(lo,hi),A=(a0,a1),the high word is UMULH(lo,a0)+lo*a1+hi*a0 modulo2^64,implemented by one UMULH and two MADD,not a wide-integer instruction. For distinct x,y with v2(x-y)=s,the difference A(x-y) is uniform over all128-bit values with valuation s. Equal high halves require its circular distance from0 to be<2^64. For s>=64 this is impossible;for s<64 the ratio is at most2^-63. Thus one compressed lane collides with probability<=3*2^-64;five independent lanes give<2^-312.
