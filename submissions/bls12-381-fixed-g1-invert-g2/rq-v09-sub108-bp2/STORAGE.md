Every object is partitioned into abstract 64-bit owner address spaces (`< 2^64` bytes = `16.00 EiB` per owner) with every cross-owner byte transfer explicitly priced in `KERNELS.md`. *(Hardware modeling caveat: physical ARMv8.1-A MMUs cap VA/PA at 48 bits [52-bit from ARMv8.2-A]; direct `ldrh`/`strh`/`staddh` addressing over `2.62–15.50 EiB` owners operates under the abstract 64-bit flat-RAM instruction-count model of this benchmark.)*
