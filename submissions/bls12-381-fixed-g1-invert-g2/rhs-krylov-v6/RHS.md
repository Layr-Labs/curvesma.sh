## Truncated Krylov condition and true generator basis

Put p=ceil(D/n_eff)+1 and q=ceil(D/m_eff)+1, so L=p+q. Define theoretical witnesses

 K_p=[Y,BY,...,B^(p-1)Y], O_q=[X^T;X^T B;...;X^T B^(q-1)].

H-X asserts that each has a nonzero D-by-D minor as a polynomial in the random V entries and X weights, for the stated deterministic block assignment and fixed production B,S_tilde. This is an explicit structural heuristic, not proved by the toys. The large witness matrices/minors are NOT constructed, searched or stored by the attack. PM-Basis only computes the block sequence and actual approximant basis already priced in GENERATOR.md.

Here is why these two ranks suffice, without a separate generator-separation assumption. Use F=[sum_i S_i^T x^i;I_m] and shift(0_n,1_m), S_i=X^T B^iY. A PM row [a(x),b(x)] of shifted degree d<=p has deg a<=d and deg b<d. Its vanishing order L gives an observed recurrence for indices d..L-1. Reverse a at degree d into Q(t)=t^d a(1/t), and set v=sum_j Q_j(B)Y_j. The recurrence says O_(L-d) v=0. Since L-d>=q and O_q is injective, v=0: the row is an exact graph relation, not merely a truncated one. This includes degree0 rows, whose numerator is0.

K_p surjectivity supplies n independent monic degree-p true relations Q_j(t)=t^p e_j minus representations of B^pY_j in K_p. Their reversed graph rows have shifted degree<=p and are independent (their denominator constant matrix isI_n). Predictable shifted degree of the full PM basis therefore requires at least n basis rows of degree<=p. All such rows are exact graph relations, which have rank n because their numerator is uniquely determined by their n denominator entries. Hence exactly n PM rows have degree<=p: they are the n least-degree rows used by the algorithm.

These rows give a basis of the full true relation module, not a submodule. Any true relation Q(t) can be divided by the monic degree-p witness relations, reducing to a true remainder of degree<p. Thus the full module is generated by true relations of degree<=p. For each such relation of degree d, its reversed graph row lies in the order-L module with shifted degree<=d. Expand it in the full PM basis. Predictable degree uses only the selected rows P_i of degrees d_i<=p, with multipliers h_i satisfying deg h_i+d_i<=d. Reversing at global degree d gives

 Q(t)=sum_i [t^(d-d_i) h_i(1/t)] Q_i(t).

The multipliers in brackets are polynomials by the degree inequality. Thus the selected Q_i span every bounded-degree generator, hence the full true relation module. Their n independent denominator rows imply independence over F_r(t), so they are a module basis. The monic degree-p relations above are correctness witnesses, not additional attack computations. Write the computed generators as Q_i(t)=C_i+t R_i(t); their Horner vectors u_i=sum_j R_ij(B)Y_j obey B u_i=-Y C_i^T.

## Recovery without a nonzero-Schirokauer premise

Write Q_i(t)=C_i+t R_i(t), C_i a row of length n, and u_i=sum_j R_ij(B)Y_j. Then B u_i=-Y C_i^T. Split C=[C_S|C_V] at its first ten columns. Compute a basis W of the left nullspace of C_V. For each row w of W form

 u=sum_i w_i u_i, s=w C_S, a=(u_firstN,s).

B u=-S_tilde s, hence alpha M u_firstN=-alpha S s and A a=0. Return the first nonzero a after verifying the original matrix relation. Zero outputs are skipped. This handles both rank M=N and rank M=N-1.

Here is an exact existence argument, conditional only on obtaining a true relation-module basis and on controllability, both supplied by H-X. Let phi:F_r[t]^n -> F_r^D send q to sum_j q_j(B)Y_j. It is surjective. Its kernel R has basis the rows Q_i. Let pi be reduction modulo Im B. The row space of C equals ker(pi Y): one direction follows from each true relation. Conversely, if Yc=Bv, express v=phi(p) by surjectivity; then c-t p lies in R, giving c in the row space of Q(0).

Let g=dim ker B=D-rank M (10 or11). Thus rank C=n-g. The map ker_left C -> ker B, w -> sum_i w_i u_i, is an isomorphism. Injectivity: if its image is0, q=wQ has constant0 and phi(q/t)=0, so q/t=bQ; nonsingularity of the polynomial basis gives w=t b, hence constant w=0. For surjectivity, for v in ker B express v=phi(p); then t p=bQ. Taking w=b(0) gives wC=0, and p-(wQ)/t belongs to R, hence v is the indicated image.

The space W={w:wC_V=0} has dimension

 g+dim(ker(pi S_tilde)) = g+10-(rank A-rank M)=11.

Its map to ker A, w -> (u_firstN,wC_S), has kernel exactly the inverse image of the ten-dimensional padding subspace of ker B, by the preceding isomorphism. Therefore its image has dimension1 and is the full virtual-log line. At least one basis vector of W gives a nonzero output. If rank M=N this has a nonzero Schirokauer part; if rank M=N-1 it may have zero Schirokauer part and nonzero ideal part. No additional H3 assumption on those coordinates is needed. Any proportional nonzero virtual-log vector gives the same ratio of the two target logarithms.
