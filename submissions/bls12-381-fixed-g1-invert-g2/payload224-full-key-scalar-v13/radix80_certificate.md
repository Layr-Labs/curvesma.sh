## Exact native-word digits and COUNT frames

Let w0,w1,w2 be the first three little-endian64-bit metadata words;only w2's low32 bits belong to the fingerprint. For pass0 the96-bit digit has
H=(w1>>1)&(2^31-1),
packed=w0+((w1&1)<<64),
M=packed>>33,
B=packed&(2^33-1).
