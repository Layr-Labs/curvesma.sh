## Finite collision probability, conditioning on all indices

For a!=0, ideal A_j=1+a*v_j and B_k=a*w_k are independent uniform labeled Fr variables. Put x_t=A_Jt+B_Kt. Conditional on ALL index pairs I, a valid E_i has probability q=11(r-1)/r^2: distinct types give independent uniform raw pairs even if one index is shared. An invalid same-type E_i is identically false. Let C=sum1[E_i], n=binom(L,2), lambda_I=E(C|I), lambda0=n*q. Then lambda_I<=lambda0 and E(lambda0-lambda_I)=lambda0/W^2.

Conditioning on I is essential. Give E_i a dependency neighborhood containing itself and all E_j sharing any A label or any B label. It is jointly independent of all indicators outside that neighborhood, since those use disjoint independent table entries. Thus b3=0. With p_i=P(E_i|I), let b1=sum_i sum_{j in N_i} p_i*p_j and b2=sum_i sum_{j in N_i,j!=i} P(E_i E_j|I). The finite Chen-Stein theorem bounds the error at zero by at most2(b1+b2). Primary theorem1: Arratia, Goldstein, Gordon, Statistical Science5(4),1990, pp403-434, https://dornsife.usc.edu/larry-goldstein/wp-content/uploads/sites/221/2023/06/pacs-1.pdf . We deliberately retain the looser factor2.

Here is the complete averaged bound, with B_i=binom(L,i):

    E(b1+b2) <= Bstar
    Bstar = 121*((B_2+12*B_3+96*B_4/W)/r^2
                  +6*B_3/(r*W^2)+24*B_4/(r*W^4)).

For b1, self pairs contribute<=121B_2/r^2. Ordered distinct event pairs have6B_3 three-time configurations and6B_4 four-time configurations. Three-time pairs always neighbor; disjoint-time pairs neighbor with probability<=8/W, by the four cross A equalities and four cross B equalities. Since p_i<=11/r, b1 contributes at most121*(B_2+6B_3+48B_4/W)/r^2.

For b2 expand the at most121 phase pairs. Each equation is a row in labeled A/B variables with coefficients1,-alpha or, at a repeated label,1-alpha; all are nonzero. Rank2 gives probability<=1/r^2. Rank1 requires identical nonzero support in BOTH families. In a three-time configuration the two other endpoints must share both labels, probability W^-2. In four times each family permits at most two ordered support matches, giving at most4/W^4 across both families. A singleton support requires all four labels equal and is already covered by those pair matches. These necessary conditions safely overcount rank1. Thus ordered three-time pairs contribute<=121*6B_3*(r^-2+(rW^2)^-1). For four times, restrict the rank2 term to neighbors (probability<=8/W), then add the rank1 upper bound: <=121*6B_4*(8/(r^2 W)+4/(rW^4)). Adding gives Bstar. This uses only two collision equations at a time; higher-order independence of collision events is never presumed.

The theorem and the1-Lipschitz function exp(-x) on x>=0 give

    epsilon=2*Bstar+lambda0/W^2 < 7*10^-17.
