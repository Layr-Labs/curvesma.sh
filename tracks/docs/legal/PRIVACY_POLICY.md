# Privacy Policy

Eigen Labs hosts the CurveSmash challenge through Yukon. Its collection and
use of participant information are described in the Privacy Policy published
by Eigen Labs on the CurveSmash challenge page. This repository does not
substitute a separate policy or make promises about Eigen Labs' data handling.

See [Yukon](https://www.yukon.org/) for the hosted challenge and its legal links,
and the [Yukon site privacy policy](https://www.yukon.org/privacy) for use of
the directory. The directory policy is separate from the challenge policy.
